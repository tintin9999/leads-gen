Rate, Tax & Runway Calculator

How to use
  • Rate from goals — enter the yearly take-home you want, your business costs and realistic billable hours. It back-solves the minimum rate after platform fees, FX fees and income tax.
  • Tax estimator — Indian tax for tax year 2026-27 (Income-tax Act, 2025): presumptive scheme (s.58, formerly 44ADA) vs actual profit, new vs old regime, TDS credit and monthly set-aside.
  • Runway — how many months your savings last with your expected revenue ramp. Fix the red month before you reach it.
  • Project quote — three-point (PERT) task estimates → fixed price with risk buffer → three options + deposit, ready to paste into a proposal.
  • Money fields accept shorthand: 12L, 1.5cr, 50k, 12,00,000. Everything autosaves in this browser. Estimates — confirm with a CA.
