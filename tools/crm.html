How to use
  • Every lead gets a next action + next date. The Follow-ups tab is your daily to-do list: clear Overdue first, then Today.
  • Add leads with Ctrl/⌘ K, or Data → Import CSV (output of the prospecting scripts, LinkedIn exports, spreadsheets — headers are auto-mapped, duplicates are detected by email / website / company).
  • Move leads by dragging cards, with the ⇄ button, or with Alt+←/→ on a focused card. Moving into Call or Proposal logs an activity automatically.
  • Log every touch (email, DM, call) in the lead drawer — the Dashboard compares this week's activity with your targets (Settings).
  • Score leads with the 6-question rubric in the drawer. Work high scores first; drop anything under ~30 unless it is warm.
  • Weighted pipeline = value × probability. Probabilities default by stage (editable in Settings).
  • Outreach Composer (sibling tool) can log touches straight into this CRM. Keyboard: / search, Esc closes panels.
Data stays in this browser — export a backup weekly. localStorage: fos.crm.v1
