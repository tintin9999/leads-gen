How to use
  • Pick a template on the left, pick a lead from your Pipeline CRM (or type details manually). Auto fields fill from the lead; you write the observation — one specific thing you noticed. Personalisation is what gets replies.
  • Fix every warning: unfilled {{variables}}, spammy words, length. Edit the final text freely — your edits stick until you press Reset.
  • Copy and send from your own email / LinkedIn, then press Log touch: the CRM gets an activity, last-touch date, next follow-up date, and Prospect → Contacted.
  • Follow the cadence: day 0 → 3 → 7 → 14 (breakup) → 30 (nurture). Most replies come from follow-ups, not the first message.
  • Cold email rules of thumb: under 125 words, one ask, one link, plain text, send from a real address, honour "not interested" immediately.
