Invoice Generator

How to use
  • Fill ⚙ Business details once: your name/address, PAN, GSTIN (if registered), Udyam number (if any), bank/UPI and foreign-receiving details, LUT ARN for exports.
  • Pick the mode per invoice: GST — India (CGST+SGST within Maharashtra, IGST for other states), No GST (not registered — the default until your turnover crosses the threshold) or Export (LUT) (foreign client, zero-rated, mandatory endorsement).
  • Invoice numbers auto-increment per financial year (Apr–Mar), max 16 characters, and can't repeat within a year — even for cancelled invoices. Never delete a sent invoice: mark it cancelled.
  • Use Register to track sent / paid / overdue and to export a CSV for your CA each month. For export invoices, enter the INR actually credited (from your FIRA / bank advice).
  • Print → "Save as PDF", A4. This is a helper, not tax advice — have your CA review your first invoice of each type.
Data stays in this browser — export a backup weekly. localStorage: fos.invoices.v1 · Not tax advice.
