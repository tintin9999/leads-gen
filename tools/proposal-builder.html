Proposal Builder

How to use
  • Click + New and pick an offer preset (A1–A4, B1–B3, C1). It pre-fills a proven structure — then rewrite the [bracketed] parts with what you learned on the discovery call. Highlighted placeholders are counted above the preview.
  • Send proposals only after a call, within 24–48 hours, and walk the client through it live if you can. Three options anchor the price: most clients pick the middle one.
  • Fill ⚙ Your details once (name, email, site, booking link) — {{CAL_LINK}} in any text becomes your booking link.
  • Print / Save PDF → choose "Save as PDF", A4, margins default, background graphics on. Drafts autosave in this browser; export a backup weekly.
