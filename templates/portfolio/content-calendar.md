# Content calendar: 30 post ideas + a 4-week plan

> **When to use:** every Monday, pick the week's two posts. Budget: 3 hours a week.
> **How to fill:** write from your real experience, with one concrete number or detail per post.
> **Never:** post confidential client details, or "hot takes" you wouldn't defend on a sales call.

## The 5 pillars

| Pillar | Purpose | Track |
|---|---|---|
| **B — Build stories** | Show how you think and build | A + B |
| **T — Teardowns** | Demonstrate the audit, start conversations | A (speed), B1 (security) |
| **L — Lessons** | Prove depth from real projects | B2, B3 |
| **C — Checklists** | Save-worthy, shareable | B1, A |
| **O — Opinions** | Personality, trust | all |

## Post template

```
Hook (1 line, specific, no clickbait)
↓
Context (2–3 lines: the situation)
↓
3–5 short points (what happened / what we did / what we learned)
↓
One number or concrete detail
↓
Soft CTA ("If your store's mobile LCP is above 4 s, comment 'speed' and I'll send a free teardown.")
```

## Repurposing loop (every two weeks)

1 deep article (dev.to / Hashnode / your site) → 3 LinkedIn posts → 1 X thread → 1 short Loom walkthrough → link in your email signature for two weeks.
