# Referral Partner Templates (10% finder's fee)

**Idea:** some people regularly meet your buyers and are already trusted by them: ex-colleagues, Cultus co-founders, designers, marketing agencies, CAs (chartered accountants) who serve D2C brands, Shopify app makers, product managers, and other freelancers. Give them a simple, generous, written reason to send clients your way.

**Standard offer:** a **10% finder's fee on the first project's collected revenue**, capped at **₹50,000 / $1,000** per client, paid **within 7 days of the client paying you**. Or offer reciprocal referrals if they'd rather not take money.

Placeholders: `{{FIRST_NAME}}` `{{YOUR_NAME}}` `{{PORTFOLIO_URL}}` `{{CAL_LINK}}` `{{CLIENT_NAME}}` `{{AMOUNT}}`

CRM: tag referred leads `partner:<name>` and set `source=referral`. You'll need this to pay fees correctly.

---

## 1. The ask (message to a potential partner)

```
Hi {{FIRST_NAME}},

You talk to a lot of {{founders / D2C brands / startups}}, so I wanted to ask about something that might help both of us.

I'm freelancing as a product engineer: Shopify apps and Razorpay checkout for D2C brands, and taking AI-built MVPs to production for startups. When people you know need that kind of help, I'd love to be your go-to recommendation.

To make it worth your while: if an intro becomes a paid project, I pay a 10% finder's fee on the first project (up to ₹50k), within a week of the client paying me. If you'd rather not take money, I'm just as happy to send referrals your way.

I'll treat anyone you introduce like gold, because your reputation is on the line too. Want a 2-line blurb you can forward?
```

## 2. Partner terms (send in writing once they say yes)

```
Referral arrangement: {{YOUR_NAME}} × {{FIRST_NAME}}

1. Qualifying referral: a client you introduce (by email/message cc'ing me) who was not already in my pipeline, and who signs a paid project within 6 months of the intro.
2. Fee: 10% of the amount I actually collect for the first project/SOW with that client, capped at ₹50,000 (or US$1,000). Paid by bank transfer/UPI within 7 days of the client's payment clearing. No fee on unpaid or refunded amounts.
3. Repeat work: no fee on later projects with the same client, unless we agree otherwise in writing.
4. Disclosure: if you advise this client professionally (e.g., as their CA, consultant or agency), please tell them you may receive a referral fee. I'll mention it too if asked.
5. No obligation: you're not responsible for the project, and I'm not obliged to take every intro. I'll tell you within 48 hours if it's not a fit.
6. Tax: I'll share fee details for your records. Each of us handles our own taxes.
7. Either of us can end this by message. Fees for referrals already made are still honored.
```
*Note:* keep it simple and honest. For larger or recurring partnerships, see [memo 07](../../memos/07-contracts.html) for a proper agreement, and [memo 03](../../memos/03-tax-and-money.html) on TDS/tax treatment of fees you pay.

## 4. Double-opt-in intro (ask the partner to check with the client first)

**Partner → client (the partner sends this):**
```
Hi {{CLIENT_FIRST_NAME}}, you mentioned {{PAIN}}. I know someone who's very good at exactly this (blurb below). Okay if I introduce you?
```

**Your reply once introduced (within 2 hours):**
```
Thanks {{FIRST_NAME}}! (moving you to BCC)

{{CLIENT_FIRST_NAME}}, great to meet you. {{FIRST_NAME}} mentioned {{PAIN}}. If it's useful, let's do 20 minutes this week and I'll tell you honestly whether I'm the right person. My calendar: {{CAL_LINK}}. Or tell me a time that works.
```

## 5. Keep the partner in the loop (they'll refer again)

**After the first call:**
```
Hi {{FIRST_NAME}}, had a great call with {{CLIENT_NAME}} today. We're scoping {{PROJECT}}. Thanks again for the intro. I'll let you know how it goes.
```

**When the fee is paid:**
```
Hi {{FIRST_NAME}}, {{CLIENT_NAME}}'s first payment cleared, so I've just sent your referral fee of ₹{{AMOUNT}} (10% of the first project). Ref: {{TXN_ID}}. Thank you for trusting me with your network 🙏
```

**If it didn't work out:**
```
Hi {{FIRST_NAME}}, quick update: {{CLIENT_NAME}} and I decided not to go ahead ({{SHORT_REASON — e.g., "timing / budget / better fit elsewhere, and I pointed them to X"}}). Thanks for thinking of me, I really appreciate it.
```

## 6. Who to recruit as partners (target: 10 in the first 60 days)
| Partner type | Why they meet your buyers | Offer that fits |
|---|---|---|
| Ex-Noovosoft colleagues and mentees | Hear about contract needs at their new companies | B1, B2, B3 |
| Cultus co-founders | D2C founder network | A1–A3 |
| Designers / brand studios | Clients ask "who builds this?" | A4, B1 |
| Performance-marketing agencies | Clients' stores are slow or leak checkout | A1, A3 |
| CAs / accountants serving D2C brands | Trusted advisors to brand owners | A2 (ERP/accounting integrations), A3 |
| Shopify app makers | Merchants need customization they don't offer | A2 |
| Other freelancers (design, marketing, mobile) | Cross-referrals | Reciprocal |
