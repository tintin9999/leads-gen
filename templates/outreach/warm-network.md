# Warm Network Templates (announcement + 6 DMs)

**Use:** Days 1–10 of the launch. Send these one at a time, by hand. Never mass-blast. Personalize the first line every time. Log each send in [the CRM](../../tools/crm.html) with `source=warm-network` (or `cultus-network`) and `nextDate` = +5 days.

**The one rule:** ask for **referrals and advice**, not "do you have work for me". People find it easy to refer someone and awkward to hire them.

Placeholders: `{{FIRST_NAME}}` `{{YOUR_NAME}}` `{{PORTFOLIO_URL}}` `{{CAL_LINK}}` `{{MONTH}}` `{{SPECIFIC_DETAIL}}` `{{MUTUAL}}`

> **NDA check before you post.** Your resume already names Coinbase's Deribit and Twinntax. Before naming them in public posts, confirm your Noovosoft exit papers (NDA, non-solicit) allow it. If unsure, use "a top crypto-derivatives exchange" and "a Belgian tax-filing platform (100k+ users)".

*Notes:* Post Tue–Thu morning IST. Reply to every comment within 2 hours, because the algorithm rewards early engagement. Add the post to your profile's Featured section.
