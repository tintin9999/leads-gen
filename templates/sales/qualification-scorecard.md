# Lead qualification scorecard

Score every lead **after the first real conversation** (reply thread or discovery call). It takes 2 minutes and saves days of chasing the wrong deals. Put the total in the CRM `score` field as `total × 10` (0–100).

| # | Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Fit:** is it one of my offers? | Outside my stack (native mobile, WordPress tweaks, ML research) | Adjacent: I can do it, but it isn't an offer | A1–A4 / B1–B3 / C1, done before | |
| 2 | **Pain + why now** | "Someday", curiosity | A real problem, no date | Money or deadline at stake (sale, launch, funding, outage) | |
| 3 | **Budget** | None, equity only, "best price?" | Unclear but plausible | Range stated, ≥ my Essential tier | |
| 4 | **Authority** | Can't reach the decision-maker | Influencer with access to them | Founder / budget owner on the call | |
| 5 | **Timeline + payment ability** | "Pay after launch / after funding", no deposit | Deposit OK but vague date | Clear date, 50% deposit OK, established company | |
| | | | | **Total (0–10)** | |

## Decision

| Total | Action |
|---|---|
| **7–10** | Send the three-option proposal within 24h and book a walk-through. |
| **5–6** | Offer the **paid first step only** (A1 Essential / B1 audit / B3 assessment). Don't write a big proposal. |
| **≤ 4** | Decline politely or move to `nurture`. Suggest someone better if you can (referrals come back to you). |

## Instant disqualifiers (any one = stop)

- [ ] Wants unpaid spec work or a free "test task" over ~1 hour
- [ ] Wants to pay in crypto, or to "overpay and you refund the difference"
- [ ] Pushes to move off Upwork or another marketplace before a contract
- [ ] Wants you to clone and run their repo/installer on your machine before anything is signed (→ memo 11)
- [ ] Refuses to put scope, price or terms in writing
- [ ] Asks for Aadhaar/PAN/bank login "for verification" before any contract
- [ ] Budget is clearly a tenth of the work and they won't cut scope

## Yellow flags (each one: tighten scope, add buffer, stricter terms, or walk away)

- [ ] "It's simple, should take a weekend"
- [ ] You're the third developer on the project
- [ ] More than 2 decision-makers, or a committee
- [ ] Wants daily calls or instant replies on WhatsApp at night
- [ ] Scope changed during the first call
- [ ] Bad-mouths every previous freelancer
- [ ] Wants hourly billing with a hard cap and fixed deliverables (the worst of both)

## Green flags (move faster)

- [ ] Referred by someone you trust, or a Cultus brand partner
- [ ] Shares real numbers (orders, AOV, RTO%, users) without being asked twice
- [ ] Has hired freelancers before and paid on time
- [ ] Asks about your process and how you'll measure success
- [ ] Deadline tied to money
