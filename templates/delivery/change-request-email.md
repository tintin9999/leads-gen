# Change Request — Email & Chat Templates

> **When to use:** As soon as a request falls outside the SOW (see [scope-creep-responses.md](scope-creep-responses.md) for what to say on the spot). Attach or link the formal [CR form](../contracts/change-request.md) for anything larger than 2 hours.
>
> **How to fill:** Always give **three options** (add, swap, defer), so the client chooses a trade-off instead of hearing a "no". Quote before working. Approval by email or chat ("Approved CR-03 Option A") is enough under the MSA. Don't start the work until you have it.
>
> **Never remove:** the explicit "I'll start once you approve" line.

---

## Email version

**Subject:** {{PROJECT_NAME}} — CR-{{NN}}: {{short title}} (needs your approval)

Hi {{CLIENT_FIRST_NAME}},

Thanks for the idea about {{feature}}. It makes sense because {{business reason in their words}}.

It isn't in the current scope (SOW {{SOW_NUMBER}}), so here are three ways we can handle it:

| | What happens | Cost | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A — Add it now** | I build {{feature}} in this phase | ₹/$ {{amount}} {{+GST}} | Launch moves {{date}} → {{date}} |
| **B — Swap** | We drop {{lower-priority scope item}} and build this instead | No change | No change |
| **C — Later** | Park it for Phase 2 / your Care Plan | — | No change |

**My recommendation:** {{option}}, because {{reason}}.

Reply with **"Approved CR-{{NN}} Option A/B/C"** and I'll get started. Until then I'll keep going on the current plan, so nothing slips.

Best,
{{YOUR_NAME}}

## WhatsApp / Slack short version

> Good idea. This one's outside our current scope, so 3 options:
> **A)** add now: ₹{{amount}}, launch +{{N}} days
> **B)** swap with {{item}}: no change in cost/date
> **C)** park for phase 2
> I'd go with **{{option}}**. Reply "Approved A/B/C" and I'll start. I'll also log it in the CR sheet.

## When you choose to do it free (goodwill)

> Quick one, so I've done it at no charge. Logged as CR-{{NN}} (₹0) so we have a record. Anything else of this size we'll batch into a CR so the timeline stays safe.

*Why log free work:* it shows the client the value they're getting, and it stops "you did it last time for free" becoming the rule.
