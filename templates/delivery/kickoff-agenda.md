# Kickoff Meeting — Agenda & Recap

> **When to use:** Hold this within 2 business days of the deposit clearing. It should be the client's first experience of you as a "studio", so it needs to be organised, short and specific.
>
> **How to fill:** Send the agenda 24 hours before the call along with the [onboarding questionnaire](client-onboarding-questionnaire.md) answers. Record the call (with permission). Send the recap email the same day. The recap is your paper trail for scope and responsibilities.
>
> **Never remove:** the out-of-scope readback, the dependency dates, and the recap email.

---

## Agenda (45–60 min)

| # | Time | Topic | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 min | Introductions & roles: who decides, who reviews, who pays | Names in the recap |
| 2 | 10 min | Goals & success signals (from questionnaire Q1–4); hard deadlines | Agreed definition of "done well" |
| 3 | 10 min | Scope walkthrough: SOW deliverables + **read out the out-of-scope list** | No surprises later |
| 4 | 5 min | Acceptance: how we test, 5-business-day review window, deemed acceptance | Client knows their homework |
| 5 | 5 min | Milestones, dates, payments; what moves dates (client dependencies) | Shared timeline |
| 6 | 5 min | Ways of working: channel of record, Friday update, demo cadence, response times, working hours, change requests | Rhythm set |
| 7 | 5 min | Access checklist: what's granted, what's missing, owner + date | Unblocked start |
| 8 | 5 min | Risks & unknowns: what could derail this? | Risk log seeded |
| 9 | 5 min | Next 7 days: first deliverable, first demo date | Momentum |

**Bring:** SOW, access checklist, a draft risk log, and a 1-page timeline (a Gantt-like table is fine).

## Questions that surface hidden scope
- "Walk me through what a customer or admin does, step by step, on the day this launches."
- "Who else will see this before launch, and what will they care about?" (This flushes out the CEO, legal, or marketing reviewer who appears in week 3.)
- "What's the one thing that absolutely must not break?" (For D2C it's usually checkout, COD or inventory sync. Test those first.)
- "What data from the old system must exist in the new one?"
- "If we had to cut 20% of the scope to hit the date, what goes first?"
