# Project Closeout — testimonial, case study, referral, retainer

> **When to use:** In the 1–2 weeks after acceptance, when the client is happiest (the "peak moment"). If you wait until the warranty ends, they've moved on. Every project should end with four assets: a **testimonial**, a **case study** (or an anonymised one), **one referral conversation**, and a **retainer offer**. This is how a freelancer with no portfolio builds one quickly.
>
> **How to fill:** Run the closeout call, then send the emails below *in this order*, a few days apart. Log everything in the [CRM](../../tools/crm.html) and set nurture reminders.
>
> **Never skip:** the ask. Clients rarely volunteer testimonials or referrals, and most say yes when asked directly and specifically.

---

## 1. Closeout call (30 min, after handover)
1. **Results:** compare the success signals agreed at kickoff with what happened. Bring numbers (Lighthouse before/after, checkout completion, admin time saved, incidents).
2. **What worked / what could be better:** your process, communication, scope.
3. **What's next for them:** roadmap, upcoming sale season, hiring plans. This is where retainer and phase-2 opportunities come from.
4. **The asks** (say them on the call, then follow up in writing): testimonial, case study permission, referral, care plan.

## 2. Testimonial request (send within 48 h of the call)
**Subject:** Quick favour? (2 minutes)

> Hi {{NAME}}, thanks again for the call. It was great to see {{result}}.
>
> Would you be open to a short testimonial I can use on my site and LinkedIn? These three prompts make it easy:
> 1. What was the situation before we worked together?
> 2. What did working with me feel like? (communication, speed, quality)
> 3. What changed afterwards? A number helps if you have one.
>
> If it's easier, here's a draft based on what you said on our call. Edit freely or just reply "approved":
> *"{{Draft: e.g. 'Our store felt slow on mobile before the festive sale. Parth audited every app and script, shipped fixes in 10 days without breaking checkout, and kept us updated every Friday. Mobile performance went from 31 to 68 and our team finally trusts our site again.' — {{Name}}, {{Role}}, {{Brand}}}}"*
>
> Can I use your name, role and company logo with it? A LinkedIn recommendation would be amazing too: {{your LinkedIn URL}}.

**Where to put it:** website case study, LinkedIn "Featured", proposals, the platform profile (Upwork/Contra), your outreach templates.

## 3. Case-study permission + interview (send ~3 days later)
> Hi {{NAME}}, one more ask: may I write a short case study about {{project}}? I'll send you the draft before anything is published, and I can anonymise your brand name or figures if you prefer ("a Pune-based D2C fashion brand").

**Interview questions (15 min, or async):**
1. What triggered the project? What had you tried before?
2. Why did you choose to work with me rather than an agency or a full-time hire?
3. What was the hardest part from your side?
4. What measurable change have you seen? (Before → after.)
5. What would you tell a peer who's considering a similar project?

**Case study structure:** Problem → Constraints → Approach (with 1–2 technical decisions and why) → Results (numbers) → Quote → Stack. Template: see [../portfolio/](../portfolio/) and [09-portfolio-and-brand](../../memos/09-portfolio-and-brand.html).

## 4. Referral ask (send ~1 week later, or include in the call)
Be specific about who to refer. "Let me know if anyone needs help" never works.

> Hi {{NAME}}, I'm taking on {{1–2}} more projects in {{month}}. I work best with **{{D2C brands on Shopify doing ₹1–20 Cr / early-stage SaaS teams with a prototype that needs hardening}}**.
>
> Is there a founder or ops lead in your network who's dealing with {{slow store / checkout or COD issues / a messy admin panel / an AI-built MVP they're nervous to launch}}? If so, I'd be grateful for an intro. Here's a two-line blurb you can forward as-is:
>
> *"{{YOUR_NAME}} is a product engineer who built {{our X}}. He specialises in Shopify/Next.js commerce and internal tools, and he's fast and very clear on communication. Worth a 20-minute chat: {{CAL_LINK}}"*

**Thank-you:** always thank the referrer, whether or not the lead converts. Offer a referral reward per your programme ([05-lead-generation](../../memos/05-lead-generation.html)), e.g. a gift or a fee on the first invoice.

## 5. Retainer / care plan offer
> Since {{store/app}} is now central to your {{revenue/operations}}, here's how I can stay on to keep it fast, secure and up to date. You'd get monitoring, updates, fixes and small improvements, with guaranteed response times. Plans start at {{₹25,000 / $1,000}} a month, minimum 3 months. Want me to send the details for {{tier}}?

Plans: [../contracts/retainer-agreement.md](../contracts/retainer-agreement.md). Best moment to offer: right before a sale season or launch, or when they mention hiring a developer "eventually".

## 7. Nurture schedule (add to CRM)
- **+30 days:** "How's {{feature}} holding up? Anything odd in the logs?" (also the end of the warranty)
- **+60 days:** share something useful (article, new Shopify feature, a quick idea for their store)
- **+90 days:** check-in + phase-2 ideas
- **Before their next big event** (sale, launch, fundraise): "Want me to do a pre-sale health check?"
