# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

> **Template — have a lawyer review for high-value deals.** Not legal advice.
>
> **When to use:** Use this only when the prospect wants an NDA before sharing details, or when you need to see their code or data to scope the work. Don't push NDAs on prospects yourself: it slows the sale. If the prospect sends their own NDA, sign it only after checking that it contains **no** non-compete, no IP assignment of "ideas", no exclusivity, and no uncapped penalty. See [red-flag-clauses.md](red-flag-clauses.md).
>
> **How to fill:** Replace `{{…}}`. Mutual is better than one-way: you also share pricing, methods and Background IP. In Maharashtra, an NDA is an "agreement not otherwise provided for" (Art. 5(h)(B)), so stamp duty is ₹500. For a pre-sale NDA that's optional in practice. Stamp it if you'd ever need to rely on it in court.
>
> **Never remove:** the residual-knowledge clause (4.3), the "no obligation / no licence" clause (6), and the fixed term (7).

---

**This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement** is made on {{DATE}} between **{{BUSINESS_NAME}}** (sole proprietorship of {{YOUR_NAME}}, {{YOUR_ADDRESS}}) and **{{CLIENT_LEGAL_NAME}}** ({{CLIENT_ADDRESS}}). Each is a "Party". The Party sharing information is the "Discloser" and the Party receiving it is the "Recipient".

**1. Purpose.** The Parties want to evaluate a possible engagement concerning {{e.g. "development of a Shopify integration"}} (the "**Purpose**").

**2. Confidential Information** means non-public information disclosed for the Purpose, in any form, that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential. Examples: source code, product plans, credentials, customer data, pricing, and methods.

**3. Obligations.** The Recipient will:
- (a) use Confidential Information only for the Purpose;
- (b) protect it with at least reasonable care;
- (c) share it only with employees, contractors and advisers who need it for the Purpose and are bound by similar obligations;
- (d) not reverse-engineer any software or prototype shared.

**4. Exclusions.**
- **4.1** These obligations do not apply to information that:
  - (a) is or becomes public through no fault of the Recipient;
  - (b) the Recipient already knew without restriction;
  - (c) the Recipient developed independently;
  - (d) the Recipient lawfully received from a third party without restriction.
- **4.2** The Recipient may disclose information when law, a court or a regulator requires it. Where lawful, it will give the Discloser prompt notice.
- **4.3 Residual knowledge.** Nothing in this Agreement restricts either Party from using the general skills, know-how, ideas, concepts and techniques that it retains in unaided memory. This does not permit disclosing specific Confidential Information, or using the other Party's code, data or credentials.

**5. Personal data.** If personal data is shared, it will be the minimum needed. The Recipient will protect it with reasonable security safeguards, consistent with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and delete it when the Purpose ends.

**6. No obligation, no licence.** This Agreement does not oblige either Party to enter any further agreement, and grants no licence or ownership in the other Party's IP. All information is provided "as is". Each Party may work with others, including on similar projects, as long as it does not use the other's Confidential Information.

**7. Term.** This Agreement covers disclosures made within **{{12}} months** of its date. Confidentiality obligations last **{{2}} years** after each disclosure. For trade secrets and credentials, they last as long as the information stays confidential.

**8. Return or deletion.** On written request, the Recipient will return or delete Confidential Information. It may keep routine backups and copies the law requires it to keep, and those copies stay confidential.

**9. Remedies.** Each Party may seek injunctive relief for an actual or threatened breach, in addition to its other remedies.

**10. Law and jurisdiction.** Laws of India. Courts at Pune have exclusive jurisdiction. [International: {{laws of the client's country, or Singapore}}.]

**11. General.** This is the entire agreement on its subject. Any change must be in writing. It may be signed in counterparts and electronically.
