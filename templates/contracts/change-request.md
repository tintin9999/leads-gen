# Change Request (CR) Form

> **When to use:** For anything not in the SOW's scope or acceptance criteria: new features, "small tweaks" over 30 minutes, changed designs, new integrations, extra revision rounds, or a moved deadline. Log every CR, including the ones you choose to do for free. A free CR still goes in the log, marked ₹0, so the client can see what they received.
>
> **How to fill:** One CR per change. Send it with [../delivery/change-request-email.md](../delivery/change-request-email.md). An email approval ("Approved CR-03 Option A") is enough under MSA cl. 5.2. Don't start the work before approval.
>
> **Never remove:** the impact on timeline, the options, and the line saying no work starts before approval.
