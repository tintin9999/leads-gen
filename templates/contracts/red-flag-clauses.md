# Red-Flag Clauses — redlining a client's contract

> **Template — have a lawyer review for high-value deals.** Not legal advice.
>
> **When to use:** Use this whenever a client, agency or platform sends *their* agreement, NDA or vendor onboarding pack. Bigger clients almost always do. You don't need to win every point. You need to remove the clauses that could **stop you getting paid, take your reusable code, or create unlimited liability**.
>
> **How to use:** Read the contract three times:
> 1. **Money:** payment, termination, set-off.
> 2. **Ownership:** IP, non-compete, non-solicit, confidentiality.
> 3. **Risk:** warranty, liability, indemnity, insurance, liquidated damages, data.
>
> Then send tracked changes with a short, friendly cover note. The script is at the bottom. Clause wording comes from [clause-library.md](clause-library.md).

## Severity key
- 🔴 **Must change.** Walk away if they refuse.
- 🟠 **Should change.** Accept only if the deal is worth it.
- 🟢 **Nice to change.** Concede freely.

## Money

| Clause you see | Why it hurts | Counter wording |
|---|---|---|
| 🔴 "Payment within 60/90 days of invoice" or "after End Client pays us" | You finance their business. Pay-when-paid passes their credit risk to you. | "Invoices payable within 15 days." Agencies: "…irrespective of payment by the End Client." Indian buyers: "not exceeding 45 days, per s.15 MSMED Act." |
| 🔴 "Payment upon Client's satisfaction" | You get paid only if they choose to pay. | "Payment on acceptance per objective Acceptance Criteria; deemed acceptance after 5 Business Days or on production use." |
| 🔴 No deposit, with payment on final delivery of a multi-week project | All the risk sits with you. | "40/40/20" or "monthly in arrears, capped at 1 month's exposure." |
| 🟠 "Client may withhold payment for any dispute" | They can freeze the whole invoice over a typo. | "Client may withhold only the disputed portion, notified in writing within 5 days; undisputed amounts paid on time." |
| 🟠 Set-off rights ("deduct any amounts owed to Client") | Unilateral deductions. | "No set-off without Consultant's written consent." |
| 🟠 "Rates fixed for the term" on a multi-year MSA | No inflation adjustment. | "Rates reviewable annually with 30 days' notice." |
| 🟠 Most-favoured-customer pricing | You can never discount for anyone else. | Strike it. |
| 🟢 "Invoices must quote PO number / be uploaded to vendor portal" | Admin overhead. | Accept, but ask for the PO before work starts. No PO, no start. |
| 🟠 TDS/GST wording missing or wrong | You lose cash flow, or get TDS deducted on GST. | Add the GST/TDS clause (clause library §5). |

## Ownership & freedom

| Clause you see | Why it hurts | Counter wording |
|---|---|---|
| 🔴 "All IP vests in Client upon creation" | They own the work even if they never pay. | "Upon receipt of full payment, Consultant assigns…" (clause library §7). |
| 🔴 IP assignment that includes "all pre-existing materials", "tools" or "know-how" | You lose your reusable toolkit. | "Excludes Background IP, which is licensed non-exclusively (§8)." |
| 🟠 Indian assignment silent on term/territory | Defaults to **5 years** and **India only** (Copyright Act s.19(5)–(6)), which hurts *them* later. Point it out: it builds trust. | Add "full term of copyright, worldwide, no lapse under s.19(4)". |
| 🔴 Non-compete ("shall not provide services to any competitor for X years") | Kills your niche. Void in India under s.27, but it still chills clients and creates threats. | Strike it. Offer confidentiality plus a 6-month staff non-solicit (§18). |
| 🟠 "Shall not work for any client of Company" (agency) for 2–3 years, all clients | Blocks you from a huge part of the market. | Narrow it to End Clients you worked with, 12 months, with a release fee (§17). |
| 🟠 Perpetual confidentiality over "all information" with no exclusions | Impossible to comply with. | 3 years post-term; standard exclusions; residuals clause (§19). |
| 🟠 No portfolio rights / total publicity ban | No case studies, so no future leads. | "May list name/logo unless Client opts out; case studies with approval" (§10). |
| 🟢 Moral rights waiver | Normal practice. | Accept "to the extent permitted by law". |
| 🔴 Exclusivity ("Consultant shall work exclusively for Client") without a retainer | It's employment without a salary. | Strike it, or price it at +25–50% with a minimum monthly fee (§18). |
| 🔴 Fixed hours, company laptop, "reporting manager", leave approval | Signals employment (and misclassification risk for them). | Independent-contractor clause (§20); availability windows instead of fixed hours. |

## Risk

| Clause you see | Why it hurts | Counter wording |
|---|---|---|
| 🔴 Unlimited liability / no cap | One incident during a sale or launch could bankrupt you. | Cap = fees paid under the SOW in the past 12 months; no consequential loss (§14). |
| 🔴 "Indemnify, defend and hold harmless against any and all claims arising from the Services" | Uninsured, unlimited insurance you're giving away. | A narrow copyright indemnity for your own code only, subject to the cap (§15). |
| 🔴 Liquidated damages ("1% of contract value per day of delay") | Delays are usually the client's fault, and LDs compound. | Delete. Or cap at 5–10% of the milestone fee, excuse client-caused delays, and treat LDs as the sole remedy for delay. In India, courts allow only *reasonable compensation* (s.74), but don't rely on a court. |
| 🟠 "Consultant warrants software is free from errors/defects" | No software meets this. | "Materially conforms to Acceptance Criteria for 30 days; sole remedy fix or refund" (§13). |
| 🟠 "Consultant shall maintain professional indemnity insurance of $1–2M" | Costs money, and individual Indian freelancers rarely hold PI cover. | Ask to waive it for small deals, or get a quote and add the premium to the price. |
| 🟠 Uncapped liability for data breaches + "Consultant responsible for all security" | You don't control their infrastructure or their staff. | "Liable only to the extent caused by Consultant's breach of its security obligations; capped at 2× Fees." |
| 🟠 Acceptance "at Client's sole discretion" with no deadline | Endless revisions and a final payment that never arrives. | Objective criteria, 5-day review, deemed acceptance (§11). |
| 🟠 "Unlimited revisions" | Scope never closes. | 2 rounds; then Change Requests (§12). |
| 🟠 Termination for convenience "without any liability" | They can cancel the day before the final milestone and owe nothing. | Pay for work done + non-cancellable costs + kill fee (§16). |
| 🟠 Unilateral arbitrator appointment ("Client's MD shall appoint the sole arbitrator") | Not neutral. The Supreme Court (*CORE*, 2024) frowns on these clauses. | "Sole arbitrator appointed by mutual agreement, failing which under s.11." |
| 🟢 Governing law/courts in the client's city or country | Inconvenient, but you won't litigate a small deal anyway. | Accept for small deals. Insist on the deposit instead. |
| 🟢 Audit rights | Rarely used. | "On 15 days' notice, once a year, at Client's cost, limited to records relating to Fees." |
| 🟠 "Background verification / submit Aadhaar, PAN, bank statements" | Data exposure; also a common scam pattern. | Share a masked Aadhaar and PAN only after verifying the company; never share OTPs or bank logins. See [11-risks-and-scams](../../memos/11-risks-and-scams.html). |

## Platform & process

| Situation | Why it hurts | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 🔴 Upwork client asks to "move off Upwork to save fees" | Breaches Upwork's non-circumvention rule (24 months from first contact unless the conversion fee is paid), and your account can be suspended. It's also a common scam opener. | "Happy to keep working with you. We can continue here, or use Upwork's official contract conversion option." |
| 🔴 "Do a free test task first" (more than 1–2 hours, or real production work) | Free labour, sometimes a scam. | Offer a **paid** trial (a 1-week mini-SOW) or point to your case studies. |
| 🔴 Client "repo to run for the interview/test" from an unknown company | Fake-recruiter malware campaigns target developers through exactly these repos. | Run unknown code only in a disposable VM or container, never on your main machine. See [11-risks-and-scams](../../memos/11-risks-and-scams.html). |
| 🟠 Vendor onboarding forms asking for an "employee" relationship | Misclassification risk. | Clarify that you're an independent contractor (GST/PAN vendor). |

## Cover-note script for your redline

> Hi {{NAME}}, thanks for sending the agreement. It's mostly standard, and I've accepted the majority as-is. I've made a few changes, each with a short comment in the margin:
> 1. **Payment:** 50% to start, balance on acceptance, Net 7. That's how I schedule capacity.
> 2. **IP:** the code is fully yours once paid. I've added the Indian Copyright Act wording (full term, worldwide) so the assignment doesn't default to 5 years and India-only. That protects you.
> 3. **Liability:** capped at the project fee, which is standard for independent engineers.
> 4. **Non-compete:** removed (unenforceable in India under s.27). Strict confidentiality and a 6-month non-solicit of your team remain.
>
> Happy to jump on a 15-minute call if anything needs discussion. If it all looks fine, I can start on {{DATE}} once the deposit is in.
