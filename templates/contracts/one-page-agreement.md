# One-Page Project Agreement (small jobs)

> **Template — have a lawyer review for high-value deals.** Not legal advice.
>
> **When to use:** One-off jobs up to about **₹50,000 / $1,000** where a full MSA + SOW would cost more effort than the job is worth. Typical uses: an A1 Speed Sprint, a B1 audit, a bug-fix block, or a paid discovery call. It can go out as a PDF for e-signature, or be pasted into an email with "Reply 'I agree' to confirm". An email agreement is a valid contract in India (*Trimex v Vedanta*, SC 2010). Still save the thread.
>
> **How to fill:** Replace every `{{…}}`. Put the scope in the box, or link your accepted proposal. For anything bigger, or for a second project with the same client, move to the MSA.
>
> **Never remove:** Payment upfront, IP-on-payment (with the full-term/worldwide wording), liability cap, no non-compete.

**3. Timeline.** Delivery by {{DATE}}, provided access and inputs arrive by {{DATE}}. Client delays move the date day for day.

**4. Acceptance.** Client reviews within **5 business days** of delivery and either accepts or lists specific failures against the scope. Silence, or use in production, counts as acceptance. Consultant fixes listed failures once at no charge. New requests are change requests.

**5. Ownership.** On **full payment**, Consultant assigns to Client all copyright in the work created for this project, for the full term of copyright, worldwide, in all media, with no lapse under s.19(4) of the Copyright Act, 1957. The fee is full consideration. Consultant keeps its pre-existing tools and code ("Background IP") and grants Client a perpetual licence to use them as part of the deliverables. Open-source parts stay under their own licences. Until full payment, Client may use the work only for review.

**6. Confidentiality.** Each side keeps the other's non-public information confidential for 3 years, and credentials and personal data for as long as they stay confidential. Consultant will not put Client secrets or personal data into AI tools. Consultant may name Client as a client unless Client opts out. Case studies need Client approval.

**7. Warranty and liability.** Consultant fixes defects against the scope reported within **{{14}} days** of acceptance. There are no other warranties, and no guarantee of business results. Neither side is liable for indirect loss or lost profits. Each side's total liability is capped at **the fee paid**, except for Client's duty to pay, and fraud.

**8. Ending early.** Either side may end this agreement by email. Client then pays for work done to date (the upfront payment counts towards this). Consultant delivers the work in progress once that is paid.

**9. Independence.** Consultant is an independent contractor. Nothing in this agreement restricts Consultant from working for anyone else, including Client's competitors.

**10. Law.** Laws of India. Courts at Pune have jurisdiction [international clients: {{choose — e.g. laws of Singapore, courts of Singapore}}]. Electronic signatures and email acceptance are binding.
