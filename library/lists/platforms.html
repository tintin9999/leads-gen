Platforms & Talent Networks: Where an India-Based Product Engineer Gets Paid (as of Nov 2026)

lists/platforms.md
Open .md source
On this pageAt a glanceDetailsApply in this orderUpwork rules that bite new freelancersSources

BLUF. Platforms add to your pipeline. They can't be the whole pipeline. Concretely:

  • Run Upwork seriously for 8 weeks (45 min/day) to bank 3–5 reviews.
  • Get vetted by Arc.dev → Flexiple → Braintrust → Toptal, in that order of effort.
  • Keep a free Contra profile as a second showcase.
  • Join the Shopify Partner and Razorpay Partner programs for credibility and tooling.
  • Skip Gun.io and Lemon.io (they lean away from India) and Fiverr (20% cut, race to the bottom).

Verify fees on the live pages before relying on them. They change often, and some figures below come from third parties (marked).

Details

Upwork: upwork.com

  • Service fee: variable, 0–15% per contract, shown before you submit a proposal and locked for the life of that contract (official). Third parties report ~10% as typical.
  • GST: India-based freelancers pay 18% GST on Upwork's fee, so a 10% fee costs ~11.8% of gross.
  • Connects: $0.15 each, minimum 10. Basic accounts get 10 free per month. Freelancer Plus: $19.99/mo with 100 Connects. Each job shows its own Connect cost, and boosts cost extra.
  • Direct Contracts (clients you bring yourself): 5%, or 0% with Plus. Escrow without the marketplace.
  • Non-circumvention: for 24 months after meeting a client on Upwork, payments must stay on Upwork unless the conversion fee is paid. Upwork Help describes it as 13.5% of estimated 12-month earnings, with reductions possible. Violations risk permanent suspension.
  • Profile review: typically about 24 hours, per Upwork staff. Generic profiles get rejected, so be specific.
  • Rates: Upwork's React page shows a median of $63/hr (typical $51–75) across all locations. Many India profiles sit at $13–37.
  • Use for: B1/B2/B3 and Shopify/Razorpay jobs (A2/A3). Templates: upwork-and-marketplaces.md.

Contra: contra.com

  • 0% commission for freelancers. The client pays $29 per contract (one-time) or $29/month (ongoing), per official client help.
  • Pro: ~$29/mo or $199/yr (third-party). Reports conflict on whether Pro is needed to apply to jobs, so check in-app.
  • Use for: a free portfolio and services page. Don't buy Pro until the profile gets views.

Arc.dev: arc.dev

  • Accepts India. It runs India talent pages and lists many India-based developers.
  • Free for talent. Profile + technical vetting.
  • Rates: Arc's React data shows a global median of $61–80 and an average of $81–100 across all levels and locations. Freelance pricing is reported at $15–110+/hr (unverified).
  • Use for: B2 contracts and contract-to-hire. Fastest vetted option.

Flexiple: flexiple.com

  • India-founded vetted network. Self-described multi-step vetting ("6-step" / "50-point"). The client pays.
  • Rates: $30–100/hr (SEO pages, unverified).

Braintrust: usebraintrust.com

  • Talent keeps 100%. The client pays a 15% success fee (official FAQ).
  • Enterprise-heavy and slower to match. Apply early.

Toptal: toptal.com

  • Screening (official FAQ): language & personality screen → in-depth skill review → live screening → test project. Takes 2+ weeks. Markets itself as the "top 3%".
  • Free for talent. The client pays Toptal's markup (estimated 30–60%, third-party).
  • Client-side rates: ~$80–120/hr mid-level (third-party). Your share is lower.
  • Prep 10–14 days before applying: timed DSA problems, a live React build, explaining trade-offs out loud.

A.Team: a.team

  • Invite/referral-based. Request access → application → interview + work-sample review by a specialist.
  • Whether it's taking new members in 2026 is unclear. A member referral helps a lot.

Wellfound and YC Work at a Startup: wellfound.com · workatastartup.com

  • Many "Remote • India" roles. Teams without an Indian entity often hire contractors.
  • Pitch "contract while you hire" for first-frontend-engineer roles.

Cutshort / Instahyre: cutshort.io · instahyre.com

  • Indian startup hiring, mostly full-time. Set "open to contract".

Shopify Partner Program: revenue share · Directory eligibility

  • Free. Gives you development stores and collaborator access to client stores, which is the professional way to work on a merchant's store.
  • App revenue share: 0% on the first $1M of lifetime gross app revenue (counted from 1 Jan 2025; you must register for the reduced plan), then 15% above that. The default is 20% if you don't register.
  • Partner Directory: tier-based and largely invite-only. The Plus tier requires about $500k/yr in revenue to Shopify from merchants you brought in. Not a year-1 channel.

Razorpay Partner Program: razorpay.com/partners

  • Free. Commission on the processing volume of merchants you onboard. Per the FAQ, payout starts once you have a minimum of 3 sub-merchants.
  • Every A3 client you set up on Razorpay can become a small recurring income stream. Disclose it to the client.

Mercor and Turing (bridge income): work.mercor.com · turing.com

  • Mercor's India-tagged listings had a median of ~$21/hr (vs $80 for US-tagged) on 28 Sep 2026. Expert coding roles pay $70–110/hr globally.
  • Turing advertises $40–100+ (third-party), and India rates are often lower.
  • Bridge only. See bridge-income.md.

Topmate: topmate.io/fees

  • 10% on bookings through your own link, 20% on marketplace-sourced buyers, plus a 2% transaction fee. No subscription.

Fiverr / Fiverr Pro: fiverr.com

  • 20% of every order, tips included. Pro sellers pay the same 20%.
  • Web-dev gigs are heavily undercut. At most, list one A1 gig.

Gun.io and Lemon.io: skip

  • Gun.io favors US/Canada engineers. Vetting is a coding assessment + resume review + live interview, with client rates around $100–200/hr.
  • Lemon.io listings exclude "most Asian countries", so India eligibility is unclear. Only apply if they confirm.

Freelancer.com, PeoplePerHour, Guru, Truelancer

  • Not researched in depth. Budget-heavy bidding markets. Skip for the first 90 days.

Upwork rules that bite new freelancers

  • Gross up your rate: net = gross × (1 − 1.18 × fee). The 1.18 is GST on the fee.
  • Don't move a client off Upwork within 24 months unless you pay the conversion fee. Even asking can get flagged.
  • Direct Contracts let you run your own clients through Upwork's escrow (5%, or 0% with Plus). Useful for overseas clients you're unsure about.
  • Connects budget: $20–40/month for the first 2 months, or Freelancer Plus if you send more than ~10 proposals/week.
  • Job filters that save Connects: payment verified · hire rate > 50% · spent > $1k · posted < 2 hours ago · fewer than 10 proposals · a scope you can do in your sleep.
