BLUF. The positioning anchors hold. Global $40–60/hr sits just under the Upwork React median ($63; typical $51–75) and well above what most India-based React freelancers charge ($20–50). That is the right starting band for a profile with zero reviews. Plan to cross $60/hr by month 6. India ₹1,500–2,500/hr is at the top of the domestic market. Don't quote it as an hourly rate to small D2C brands. Sell fixed packages (A1–A4) and qualify for brands that can pay.

Figures are benchmarks, not promises. Most public rate data comes from vendor blogs or marketplaces with their own agenda. Reliability is marked per row: High = official page of the platform itself · Med = reputable aggregator or platform data with unclear method · Low = SEO/vendor blog.

FX assumption used throughout the repo's lead-gen docs: ₹95 = $1. This is a mid-2026 level: Wise showed ~₹94.85, and Pluang showed ₹96.5 on 22 Jul 2026 with a 52-week range of ₹86–97. Check the live rate before quoting.

Rate ladder on marketplaces (Upwork)

  1. Contracts 1–3: small fixed-price jobs ($150–500) or $35–40/hr, picked to earn reviews fast.
  2. Contracts 4–8: $45–50/hr, plus Project Catalog packages.
  3. After 5+ five-star reviews and a strong Job Success Score: $55–70/hr. Raise by $5–10 every 2–3 contracts until win rate drops below ~15%.

Gross-up for platform costs

Upwork charges a variable 0–15% service fee per contract, plus 18% GST on that fee for India-based freelancers. Net = gross × (1 − 1.18 × fee).

Fee shown on the jobYou keepTo net $40/hr, bill

Other platforms: Fiverr 20% → you keep 80%. Contra 0% (the client pays a $29 contract fee). Braintrust 0% (the client pays 15%). Toptal/Arc: you set your rate and the client pays the markup. Payment-rail fees (Skydo, Wise, Payoneer, bank SWIFT) come on top. See memo 03 — Tax & Money. Pricing method and proposal tiers: memo 04 — Pricing.

4. Flags on the brief's anchors

  • India ₹1,500–2,500/hr is top-of-market. Fine for brands doing ₹1Cr+ GMV/yr with money-critical work (A2/A3). Too high for a 2-person brand doing ₹5L/month: you'll hear "my nephew does it for ₹10k". Qualify first, and never discount below the ₹1,200 floor. Cut scope instead.
  • Global $40–60 is slightly conservative next to Upwork's React median ($63) and Arc's median ($61–80). That's deliberate while you have no reviews. Re-price once you have 3 case studies + 3 testimonials, not "after a year".
  • Global work pays 2–3× per hour. At ₹95/$, $50/hr ≈ ₹4,750/hr vs ₹1,500–2,500 domestic. Track A exists for speed to first rupee and proof, Track B for rate growth. Shift your hours toward B as soon as B converts.
