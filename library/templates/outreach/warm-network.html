Warm Network Templates (announcement + 6 DMs)

Open the .md file

Use: Days 1–10 of the launch. Send these one at a time, by hand. Never mass-blast. Personalize the first line every time. Log each send in the CRM with source=warm-network (or cultus-network) and nextDate = +5 days.

The one rule: ask for referrals and advice, not "do you have work for me". People find it easy to refer someone and awkward to hire them.

Placeholders: {{FIRST_NAME}} {{YOUR_NAME}} {{PORTFOLIO_URL}} {{CAL_LINK}} {{MONTH}} {{SPECIFIC_DETAIL}} {{MUTUAL}}

NDA check before you post. Your resume already names Coinbase's Deribit and Twinntax. Before naming them in public posts, confirm your Noovosoft exit papers (NDA, non-solicit) allow it. If unsure, use "a top crypto-derivatives exchange" and "a Belgian tax-filing platform (100k+ users)".

1. LinkedIn announcement post (Day 1)

After 4 years at Noovosoft, building back-office tools for Coinbase's Deribit and a tax-filing module used by 100,000+ people in Belgium, I'm now independent.

What I do: I build and fix the parts of a product where mistakes cost money.

→ D2C brands on Shopify: faster stores, custom Shopify apps and integrations, Razorpay checkout and COD that doesn't leak orders.
→ Startups: taking AI-built or rushed MVPs to production (auth, Supabase security, payments, tests, CI), plus internal admin tools your ops team will actually use.

Proof I can point to: I built Cultus (cultus.co.in) end to end: Next.js + Supabase, Razorpay Magic Checkout with partial COD, and a custom Shopify Sales Channel app. First 4 months: 890+ users, 180+ orders, 12 brand partners.

I'm taking on 2 new projects for {{MONTH}}.

If you know a founder or team who needs this, an intro would mean a lot. DMs open.

Notes: Post Tue–Thu morning IST. Reply to every comment within 2 hours, because the algorithm rewards early engagement. Add the post to your profile's Featured section.

Why this wording: don't poach your ex-employer's client accounts. Even where a non-solicit clause may not be enforceable, poaching burns your reputation and a referral source. Ask for referrals elsewhere. If you're unsure about your exit terms, get advice (memo 07).
