Upwork profile kit

Open the .md file

When to use: when you build or update your Upwork profile. How to fill: copy a block, then change any line that is not exactly true for you. Never do: put an email, phone number, social handle, your website link or a contact form anywhere in the profile.

1. Title (pick one; 70 characters at most)

  1. Next.js & Supabase Developer | I Fix and Finish Web Apps
  2. Next.js, React & Supabase | Login, Payments and Admin Dashboards
  3. Fix Your AI-Built App | Next.js & Supabase Production Developer

Start with 1. Try 3 if most replies come from AI-builder jobs.

2. Overview

Short version (the first lines clients see in search)

I fix and finish web apps built with Next.js, React and Supabase. I built Cultus, a live fashion marketplace with 890+ users and 180+ paid orders in its first four months.

Long version (paste the whole thing)

I fix and finish web apps built with Next.js, React and Supabase. I built Cultus, a live fashion marketplace with 890+ users and 180+ paid orders in its first four months.

What I can do for you:

  • Fix login and sign-up bugs, and close data-access holes in Supabase.
  • Fix checkout and payment flows (Stripe or Razorpay).
  • Build admin dashboards your team can use every day.
  • Take apps built with Lovable, Bolt, v0 or Cursor and make them ready for real users.

My background: 4 years as a full-time developer. I built back-office tools for a large crypto exchange, and the tax-filing module of a platform used by 100,000+ people. Cultus has 115 test files on its money paths.

How I work: you get a short written plan before I start. You get an update every working day. I test the money paths before I hand over, and you get plain-English notes.

Send me a link to your app and tell me what is broken. I will tell you what I would fix first.

Name your old employer's clients only if your old agreement allows it.

3. Skills (10 to 15, from Upwork's own list)

Next.js, React, TypeScript, Supabase, PostgreSQL, Node.js, Tailwind CSS, API Integration, Payment Gateway Integration, Stripe, Web Application, Bug Fix, Dashboard, Authentication.

Add only skills you have used in real work.

4. Portfolio items

Item 1: Cultus marketplace

  • Title: Cultus: a live fashion marketplace on Next.js and Supabase
  • Role: Built it end to end, with two co-founders.
  • Problem: Bring many fashion brands into one shop, with stock kept in sync with each brand's Shopify store.
  • What I did: Built the store, the login, the brand dashboards and a Shopify app that syncs stock every minute, with a nightly check.
  • Result: In the first four months after the April 2026 launch: 890+ users, 180+ orders, 12 brand partners, 450+ live products.
  • Attach: 3 screenshots (home, product page, brand dashboard) and the link cultus.co.in, if the site shows no personal contact details.

Item 2: Cultus checkout and payments

  • Title: Razorpay checkout with part payment on delivery
  • Role: Designed and built the payment flow.
  • Problem: Many Indian shoppers want to pay cash on delivery, which is a risk for the brand.
  • What I did: Built Razorpay Magic Checkout with a small online payment and the rest on delivery. Wrote tests for every money path.
  • Result: ₹2.9 lakh+ in sales in four months; 115 test files on the money paths.
  • Attach: Checkout screenshots and a simple flow diagram (customer, checkout, payment, order marked paid).

Item 3: Bro, a Discord bot

  • Title: Bro: a Discord bot scaled to 10,000+ servers
  • Role: Took it over, rewrote it and ran it from 2019 to 2024.
  • Problem: The bot needed a rewrite to keep growing. ({{CHANGE: one real sentence on what was wrong}})
  • What I did: Rewrote the bot and kept it running as it grew.
  • Result: 10,000+ servers and 2.4 million members.
  • Attach: A screenshot of the bot at work and one of the server count.

6. Pre-publish checklist

  • Photo: your face, in daylight, plain wall, no sunglasses.
  • Name matches your identity document.
  • Title under 70 characters, tools first.
  • Overview starts with your service and Cultus, not "Hi, I am".
  • Rate set to $40 an hour.
  • 10 to 15 skills, all used in real work.
  • Three portfolio items, each with a number in the result.
  • Searched the whole profile for "@", "http" and phone digits.
  • No link to your own website or any page with a contact form.
  • City and other required fields filled.
