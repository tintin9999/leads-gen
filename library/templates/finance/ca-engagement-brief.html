CA engagement brief: hiring and using a Chartered Accountant

Open the .md file

As of Nov 2026. Fee ranges are typical Pune quotes for a solo freelancer. Get 2–3 quotes. Background: Tax & Money · Business Setup.

BLUF. Pay a CA for three things: (1) your first return under s.58 presumptive with salary and freelance income in one year, (2) the GST decision and, when you register, the LUT and return routine, (3) an annual retainer once GST-registered. Expect ₹1.5k–5k for ITR-4 and ₹15k–40k/yr for a full retainer. Bring the questions below to a 30-minute call, hand over the year-end pack in one go, and keep your passwords.

Finding and vetting (3 quotes)

Where: referrals from freelancer friends or ex-colleagues, local CA firms that already serve exporters or IT freelancers, and ICAI's member directory to verify a membership number.

Green flags

  • Already files for freelancers or exporters. Knows s.58, LUT, FIRA, the 2026 EDF changes and the Income-tax Act 2025 renumbering.
  • Gives a fixed-fee, written scope (an engagement letter).
  • Sends a tax computation for your approval before filing.
  • Files through ERI/consent-based access or a client portal, not by asking you to email passwords.
  • Answers within a business day near deadlines.

Red flags (walk away)

  • Promises "zero tax" through invented expenses, fake rent receipts or bogus deductions.
  • Suggests filing software development under the 6%/8% business scheme to cut tax.
  • Suggests labelling foreign income as a gift or family remittance.
  • Wants to keep your e-filing password permanently. No membership number. No written scope.

First-call questions (copy and paste)

  1. My work is custom software development and consulting for Indian and foreign clients. Is it eligible for s.58 professional presumptive taxation? Which nature-of-business code will you use?
  2. In tax year 2026-27 I had salary (Apr–Sep 2026) and freelance receipts (Oct 2026–Mar 2027). Which ITR form, and what tax or refund do you expect?
  3. Do I need advance tax by 15 March 2027? How much, given TDS already deducted?
  4. GST: I expect about ₹{{EXPECTED_TURNOVER}} total turnover this year, {{FOREIGN_PCT}}% foreign. Register now or later? What's your view on Notification 10/2017-Integrated Tax for exports below ₹20L?
  5. If I register: QRMP? LUT timing? How do you handle reverse charge on foreign SaaS? Is an ITC refund on my laptop worth claiming?
  6. Purpose code for my foreign receipts: P0802 or P0807?
  7. Under presumptive taxation, cash or mercantile accounting? Does it matter near year-end?
  8. What minimum records do you want monthly or quarterly?
  9. Clients deduct TDS at 10%, 2% or 1%. Anything to do? Is a lower-deduction certificate (s.395/Form 128) worth it?
  10. Professional tax: confirm PTEC covers me, and the due date (15 or 30 June).
  11. EDF: as an individual receiving through {{RAIL}} or my bank, is anything needed beyond giving the bank remittance details?
  12. At what point should I consider an LLP or Pvt Ltd? Which triggers matter for me?
  13. Is my FY 2025-26 return filed correctly? If not, can we file a belated return before 31 Dec 2026?
  14. Fees: fixed fee for the ITR? Retainer scope? Turnaround? How do I approve before you file?

Year-end hand-over pack (send in one go, mid-April)

  • PAN, Aadhaar (masked copies). E-filing access through ERI/consent. If you must share a password, change it after filing
  • Form 130 (ex-Form 16) from Noovosoft for Apr–Sep 2026 salary
  • AIS, TIS and Form 168 (ex-26AS) PDFs
  • Invoice register for the full year, plus the receipts register (rail, gross, fees, net, purpose code)
  • FIRA for every foreign payment, named by invoice number
  • Bank statements: business, tax-reserve and personal accounts (interest income), and FD interest certificates
  • Advance-tax challans
  • TDS certificates from Indian clients
  • Expense register (not needed for s.58 tax, but useful for advice and GST ITC)
  • Capital-gains statements (stocks/MF), if any
  • Udyam, PTEC and GST certificates, plus the LUT ARN
  • Year-end balances (ITR-4 asks for them): unpaid invoices (debtors), unpaid bills (creditors), cash in hand, bank balances on 31 March
  • Last year's ITR acknowledgement and computation

Engagement letter: make sure it covers

  • Scope: ITR, GST returns, LUT, advisory hours, notices (included or extra?)
  • Fee, payment terms and what counts as out of scope
  • Timelines: you deliver documents by X, they file by Y (at least 2 weeks before the due date)
  • Your approval step before any filing
  • Access method (ERI consent, DSC) and confidentiality
  • Who handles tax notices, and at what cost
