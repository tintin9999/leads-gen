Content calendar: 30 post ideas + a 4-week plan

templates/portfolio/content-calendar.md
Open .md source
On this pageThe 5 pillars30 ideas4-week starter planPost templateRepurposing loop (every two weeks)

When to use: every Monday, pick the week's two posts. Budget: 3 hours a week. How to fill: write from your real experience, with one concrete number or detail per post. Never: post confidential client details, or "hot takes" you wouldn't defend on a sales call.

The 5 pillars

PillarPurposeTrack
B — Build storiesShow how you think and buildA + B
T — TeardownsDemonstrate the audit, start conversationsA (speed), B1 (security)
L — LessonsProve depth from real projectsB2, B3
C — ChecklistsSave-worthy, shareableB1, A
O — OpinionsPersonality, trustall

30 ideas

Build stories (B)

  1. How Cultus syncs inventory for 12 Shopify brands every minute, and why we still reconcile every night.
  2. Partial COD for risky pincodes: how we cut return-to-origin risk without killing conversion.
  3. Building a Shopify Sales Channel app: what the docs don't tell you.
  4. 115 test files on a side project? Here's exactly what we test (and what we don't).
  5. 17 ADRs later: why I write down every architecture decision.
  6. Refund retries: the boring job that saves your reputation.
  7. Scaling a Discord bot to 10 shards: three things that broke first.

Teardowns (T)

  1. I tested 100 Indian D2C stores on mobile. Here's what slows them down. (speed report)
  2. Your Shopify store has 23 apps. Here's what each one costs you in load time.
  3. The hero video that costs you sales: a 5-minute teardown of a fashion store's product page.
  4. Checkout teardown: COD vs prepaid flows in three Indian D2C stores.
  5. Teardown of my own site: from Lighthouse 71 to 98 in one evening.
  6. Anatomy of a Lovable app before launch (my own demo): 6 things I'd fix.

Lessons (L)

  1. Migrating Angular to React without a rewrite: the strangler pattern we used.
  2. Introducing tests into a codebase that has none: start with CI, not coverage.
  3. What I learned working inside a Coinbase-owned exchange's code standards.
  4. Dynamic forms for 100k users: schema-driven UI and recursive validation.
  5. Versioning business rules by year (tax forms taught me this).
  6. Mentoring 10 developers: the feedback that actually changed their code.
  7. Four months of Erlang/OTP as a React developer.

Checklists (C)

  1. Before you launch your Lovable/Bolt app: 9 things to check in Supabase.
  2. The Razorpay webhook checklist: signatures, idempotency, retries, reconciliation.
  3. Shopify store speed: a 15-minute self-check for founders.
  4. What "production-ready" actually means for an MVP (a one-page checklist).
  5. Admin dashboards: the 7 features ops teams always ask for later.

Opinions (O)

  1. AI writes most of my code now. Here's what I never let it touch.
  2. Hourly billing punishes good engineers. Why I quote fixed prices with three options.
  3. Your MVP doesn't need microservices. It needs tests on money paths.
  4. Agencies vs freelancers for D2C brands: an honest comparison.
  5. Why I went independent after four years, and what I'm building now. (announcement post)

Post template

Hook (1 line, specific, no clickbait)
↓
Context (2–3 lines: the situation)
↓
3–5 short points (what happened / what we did / what we learned)
↓
One number or concrete detail
↓
Soft CTA ("If your store's mobile LCP is above 4 s, comment 'speed' and I'll send a free teardown.")

Repurposing loop (every two weeks)

1 deep article (dev.to / Hashnode / your site) → 3 LinkedIn posts → 1 X thread → 1 short Loom walkthrough → link in your email signature for two weeks.
