Bottom line. Revenue is a lagging number. By the time it's low, the cause is six weeks old. Track the leading numbers weekly (touches, conversations, calls, proposals) and the health numbers monthly (pipeline coverage, effective hourly rate, top-client share, runway). Run a fixed rhythm: 15 touches every morning, a 45-minute CEO review every Friday, a money close on the first of each month, and a strategy review each quarter.

Funnel maths: from revenue goal to daily touches

wins needed      = monthly revenue goal ÷ average deal size
proposals needed = wins ÷ proposal win rate
calls needed     = proposals ÷ call→proposal rate
replies needed   = calls ÷ reply→call rate
touches needed   = replies ÷ reply rate

Quarterly strategy review

  • Positioning: which ICP produced the best clients (revenue, ease, referrals)? Narrow further?
  • Offers: which offer sold, which didn't? Kill or rewrite one.
  • Prices: apply the triggers in Pricing.
  • Channels: hours spent vs revenue per channel. Double the best, drop the worst.
  • Skills: one learning goal tied to an offer (e.g., Shopify Functions, Hydrogen, Postgres performance).
  • Health: hours worked, days off, sleep, isolation. Book the next vacation.

Burnout guardrails

  • Cap at 50 hours a week, including Cultus. Keep one full day off.
  • Two weeks of real vacation a year; tell clients four weeks ahead and price it into your rate.
  • Work from a coworking space or a café with people twice a week. Isolation is the quiet freelancer killer.
  • Keep a "wins" note: testimonials, thank-you messages, numbers. Read it on bad weeks.

Actions

  • Put the daily outreach and shutdown blocks in your calendar as recurring events.
  • Book a recurring Friday 45-minute "CEO review".
  • Book the first working day of each month for the money close.
  • Set the CRM targets (touches/day, calls/week, proposals/week) in its settings.
  • After 30 days, replace the planning conversion rates with your own and recalculate daily touches.
  • Pick this week's experiment.
