Most late payments come from busy or forgetful clients, not dishonest ones. This page gives you a fixed ladder of steps for Upwork and for direct clients. You follow the ladder; you do not argue.

What it looks like

Maya is a made-up founder in the US. Her app "Fernleaf" was built on Next.js and Supabase. She hired you on Upwork for a $300 fixed-price job: fix the login bug and the Stripe payment bug.

You submit the work on 3 November. Four days pass with no reply, then she writes: "Not sure the checkout fix works. Can I get a refund?"

It feels personal, but it is a process. Upwork already holds her $300, and there are set steps for each case. Stay polite, keep every message on Upwork, and take one step at a time.

Upwork, fixed price: what protects you

On a fixed-price job, the client puts the money for each step (milestone) with Upwork before work starts. Upwork holds it (escrow). Your money is already there before you write any code.

After you submit, the client has 14 days to review. Here is what happens in each case:

  • She is silent. After 14 days the payment is released to you automatically. Then there is a 5-day security wait before you can withdraw it.
  • She asks for changes. Make fair fixes that are inside the job, then submit again. The 14-day review starts again.
  • She asks for a refund. You can agree, offer part, or dispute it within 7 days. While it is disputed, Upwork keeps holding the money.

If she asks for changes, reply with facts, not feelings:

"Thanks, Maya, the checkout fix is live: test order #1042 was marked paid in Stripe and in your database. Could you tell me which step fails for you? I'll fix anything inside the job today."

The dispute steps on Upwork

If talking does not work, use Upwork's own process. As of November 2026 it runs like this:

  1. When she requests a refund, open the contract and choose to dispute it within 7 days.
  2. Upload your proof: the agreed plan from the chat, your submission, test results and screenshots.
  3. An Upwork team member then talks to you both and suggests a fair split (mediation). It is not binding.
  4. If either of you refuses that suggestion, you can both choose a paid, binding decision by an outside judge (arbitration). Each side pays a fee.
  5. For a small job like $300, the arbitration fee can cost more than the payment. Usually accept a fair mediation result.

The fee and deadlines change. Check the current rule on Upwork's help page before you decide on arbitration.

Upwork, hourly: what the tracker must record

On an hourly job, Upwork's promise to pay for your hours (Hourly Payment Protection) only covers time recorded the right way. The record of your hours is called the Work Diary.

  1. Track time only with the Upwork desktop app. Hours you type in by hand are not covered.
  2. Make sure the screenshots it takes show work for this contract.
  3. Write a short note on each block of time (memo), for example "Stripe webhook: retry test".
  4. Keep your hours within the weekly limit in the contract.
  5. Keep your identity verified and your account in good standing.

Time that is manual, empty or idle may not be covered. The client must also have a verified way to pay.

A direct client: the reminder ladder

A direct client is one you found outside a portal. Nobody holds money for you, so you take 50% before work starts. For the rest, you follow a ladder by the due date.

Asha is a made-up owner of "Indigo Thread", a Pune clothing brand on Shopify. She owes the second half of a ₹48,000 Store Speed Sprint: ₹24,000, due on 20 November.

  • 18 November (2 days before): "Hi Asha, a quick note that invoice 007 for ₹24,000 is due on 20 November. The payment link is in the PDF."
  • 20 November (due day): "Invoice 007 is due today. If it is already on its way, please ignore this."
  • 23 November (3 days late): "I haven't seen invoice 007 come through. Could you share the expected payment date?"
  • 27 November (7 days late): A firm email, plus a phone call. Ask for a date by a set day.
  • 4 December (14 days late): Written notice that work stops in 3 working days unless paid.
  • 20 December (30 days late): A final formal notice before legal steps.

Send each step by email, the record that counts. A short WhatsApp message can point to it. Never threaten anything you will not do.

The full wording for each step is in the payment reminder ladder.

Extra protection for Indian business clients

India has a free registration for small businesses (Udyam). If you are registered before you sign the work, a law protects you from late payment by Indian business clients.

The client must pay within the agreed time, and never later than 45 days. After that, interest grows at three times the bank rate set by India's central bank. That is a strong reason for her accounts team to pay.

At 7 days late, add one calm line:

"For your accounts team: I am a micro enterprise registered under Udyam. Under the law for small suppliers, late payments carry interest at three times the bank rate. I'd much rather we settle this quickly."

You do not need Udyam for Upwork work. It matters later, with Indian direct clients. See Invoice a client and collect the money.
