A client decides in about 10 seconds whether to open your profile. This page gives you a finished profile, built from your real work. Paste it, adjust it, and avoid the few mistakes that get new profiles rejected.

What a client sees in 10 seconds

Maya is a made-up founder in the US. Her app's login is broken. She searches Upwork for "Next.js Supabase developer".

In the list she sees only four things about you: your photo, your title, your rate, and the first two lines of your overview.

Your title: "Next.js & Supabase Developer | I Fix and Finish Web Apps". Your rate: $40 an hour.

Your first two lines: "I fix and finish web apps built with Next.js, React and Supabase. I built Cultus, a live fashion marketplace with 890+ users and 180+ paid orders."

She now knows what you do and that you have done it for real.

The title can be 70 characters at most, as of November 2026. The one above is 56. Put the tools first, because clients search by tool name.

The overview is the text under your title. It can be 5,000 characters, but the search list shows only about the first 250. Put your service and your best proof there.

Do not open with "Hi, I am Parth, a passionate developer". Maya learns nothing from that line.

The full overview to paste

I fix and finish web apps built with Next.js, React and Supabase. I built Cultus, a live fashion marketplace with 890+ users and 180+ paid orders in its first four months.

What I can do for you: fix login and sign-up bugs, close data-access holes in Supabase, and fix checkout and payment flows. I also build admin dashboards. I take apps built with Lovable, Bolt, v0 or Cursor and make them ready for real users.

My background: 4 years as a full-time developer. I built back-office tools for a large crypto exchange, and the tax-filing module of a platform used by 100,000+ people. Cultus has 115 test files on its money paths.

How I work: you get a short written plan before I start. You get an update every working day. I test the money paths before I hand over.

Send me a link to your app and tell me what is broken. I will tell you what I would fix first.

That is about 150 words, in short paragraphs.

Name your old employer's clients only if your old agreement allows it. Rules from your old job and from Cultus explains how to check.

Rate, skills and photo

  • Rate: $40 an hour. It is high enough to look serious and low enough for a profile with no reviews. Work out your minimum rate shows the reasoning.
  • Skills: pick 10 to 15 tags from Upwork's own list. Start with Next.js, React, TypeScript, Supabase, PostgreSQL, Node.js and Tailwind CSS. Then add API Integration, Payment Gateway Integration, Web Application and Bug Fix.
  • Photo: your real face, looking at the camera, in daylight, with a plain wall behind you. No sunglasses, no logo, no group photo.

Add only skills you have used in real work.

You can also save a case study from your website as a PDF and attach it. Remove your email address and phone number from the PDF first.

Rules that get profiles rejected

Do not put an email address, phone number, social handle or contact form link anywhere in your profile. Upwork treats this as trying to move clients off the site.

  • Outside links: a portfolio item may link to an outside page that shows your work. That page must not show your contact details or a contact form.
  • Your own website: it has a way to contact you, so do not link it. Use screenshots and the PDF instead.
  • Copied text: write your own overview. Text copied from another profile or pasted straight from an AI tool gets flagged.
  • Claims you cannot show: every number above is real and you can prove it. Keep it that way.
  • Identity: use your real name and photo. They must match your identity document.

After a contract starts, you may share contact details with that client. Before that, keep every message on Upwork.

Leave these for later

Specialised profiles were extra profiles for a second skill. Upwork removed them on 28 May 2026. You now have one profile, and Upwork shows each client your most relevant work.

So Shopify does not get a second profile. After your first reviews, add Shopify skills and one Shopify portfolio item to this profile.

The Project Catalog is a shop inside Upwork. You list a fixed package, for example "Supabase security check for $300", and clients buy it without a proposal.

Listings with no reviews sell rarely. Set one up after your first three reviews. Two drafts wait in the profile kit.

If Upwork does not approve your profile, this page gives the steps.
