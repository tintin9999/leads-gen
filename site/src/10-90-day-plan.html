Bottom line. Three phases.
  • Days 1–14, Foundation + warm launch: money and legal plumbing, offers, LinkedIn, site, contracts. Announce publicly and DM 50 people.
  • Days 15–45, Outreach engine + first wins: 15 touches a day, A1 speed teardowns, B1 posts, marketplaces. Close your first 1–2 projects.
  • Days 46–90, Systematize + shift up: referrals, retainers, case studies from real work, higher prices, more Track B.
Targets: first paid project by day 30–45, ₹1L+/month run-rate by day 90, at least one client paying in USD.

At an average ₹50k that's ₹1L a month. Warm leads convert much better, which is why the first two weeks are all warm. Funnel maths in Metrics & Cadence.

Phase 1 · Days 1–14: Foundation and warm launch

Week 1: plumbing and positioning

  • Write down monthly personal burn and savings; compute runway (calculator → Runway).
  • Buy personal health insurance (or port your group policy) if cover lapsed when you left Noovosoft.
  • Designate a separate bank account for business money. Set up UPI on it.
  • Register on Udyam (free, Aadhaar + PAN, ~20 min) — see Business Setup.
  • Decide GST status for now (see Tax & Money); book a 30-minute call with a CA if unsure.
  • Open a receiving account on a low-fee foreign payment rail that issues FIRAs (see payment rails).
  • Agree Cultus hours and the "can I pitch Cultus brands?" question with co-founders, in writing.
  • Re-read Noovosoft exit papers (NDA, non-solicit). List who you can and can't approach.
  • Pick launch offers A1 and B1; compute your MVR and three-tier prices (Pricing).
  • Rewrite LinkedIn: headline, banner, About, Featured, Experience; create the Service Page (kit).
  • Ask five people for LinkedIn recommendations.

Week 2: warm launch

  • Build the 100-person warm list in the CRM: ex-colleagues, mentees, managers, SKNCOE alumni, Cultus co-founders and brands, friends who run businesses, Bro community contacts.
  • Publish the "I've gone independent" post (template in warm-network.md).
  • Send 50 personal DMs: specific, short, one clear ask ("who do you know who…").
  • Buy the domain, set up professional email, Cal.com with 20-minute slots.
  • Ship the one-page site (copy).
  • Finish the Cultus and Bro case studies (drafts).
  • Fill the MSA, SOW and NDA templates with your details; save them as ready-to-send docs (Contracts).
  • Configure the Invoice Generator and Proposal Builder settings.
  • Run 10 free PageSpeed teardowns on friends' and Cultus-partner stores to calibrate A1.
If you're behind on day 14: skip polish, not outreach. A plain one-page site and a good LinkedIn are enough. The DMs matter more than the website.

Phase 2 · Days 15–45: Outreach engine and first wins

Week 3: daily engine on

  • Run the 15-touch morning block every working day (mix: 5 warm follow-ups, 5 Track A, 5 Track B).
  • Send 10 Loom speed teardowns to D2C brands (A1 hook).
  • Run npm run hn and reply to the best "SEEKING FREELANCER" posts.
  • Post a "SEEKING WORK" comment in the monthly HN freelancer thread (template in community-posts.md).
  • Create Contra and Upwork profiles; publish one Project Catalog item for A1 or B1.
  • Apply to one talent network (Toptal or Arc): screening takes weeks, so start now.
  • Do every discovery call with the script; send proposals within 24 hours.

Week 4: the speed report

  • Run the site audit with PageSpeed on ~100 Indian D2C stores (brief).
  • Publish the aggregate report on your site + LinkedIn post.
  • Message 25 brands with their personal results.
  • Pitch 5 agencies (design, performance-marketing, Shopify) for overflow/white-label work (template).
  • Follow up on every open proposal (day 2, day 5, day 10 cadence).

Week 5: close and deliver

  • Target: first signed SOW + deposit received. Kick off with the kickoff agenda.
  • Message the remaining 75 speed-report brands, 15 a day.
  • Start the Supabase Production-Readiness Kit (B1 spec project).
  • Send a weekly update to every active client on Friday (template).
  • Reply to every Reddit/HN/Contra lead within 24 hours.

Week 6: second win

  • Ship the B1 kit publicly (GitHub + post + builder communities).
  • Ask every warm contact who said "not now" for one introduction.
  • Target: second signed project or first USD client.
  • First monthly money close: invoices, receipts, tax reserve, runway (routine).
  • Day-45 mini review: which channel produced calls? Double it next week.
If you have zero calls by day 30: the problem is almost always the message or the list, not the market. Ask three friendly founders to read your outreach and say what they'd ignore. Narrow the ICP (one city, one category, one trigger) and make the first line about their numbers. Consider a short bridge contract while you fix it.

Phase 3 · Days 46–90: Systematize and shift up

Weeks 7–8: proof from real work

  • Deliver project 1 with the handover checklist.
  • Ask for the four assets at handover: testimonial, case study, referral, care plan (closeout).
  • Write the case study from project 1 within a week of delivery.
  • Offer a care plan to every delivered client.
  • Shift the outreach mix to 50/50 Track A/Track B.
  • Publish posts twice a week from the calendar.

Weeks 9–10: referral and partner loops

  • Set up a referral offer (10% of the first project for introductions that close).
  • Follow up with the five agencies you pitched; offer a small paid trial task.
  • Pitch B1 audits to 20 recently launched AI-built products (Product Hunt, Show HN, builder communities).
  • Second monthly money close; set aside your tax reserve.
  • Re-check talent-network applications; prepare for their technical screens.

Weeks 11–12: raise prices, tighten the system

  • If you have 3 testimonials, raise new-client prices 15–20% (rules).
  • Turn the best offer into its own landing page with three tiers.
  • Review effective hourly rate per project; fix estimates where you lost money.
  • Archive dead leads into "nurture" with a 90-day re-engagement date.
  • Draft the B2 admin-dashboard starter scope if Track B internal-tools leads appeared.

Week 13: the day-90 review

  • Run the review below (2 hours, written).
  • Decide: keep both tracks, or double down on one.
  • Set the next 90-day targets and update this plan.

If things go off-plan

Lots of interest, no closes

Proposals too big or vague. Lead with the paid audit or Essential tier; add a founding-client discount with an expiry.

Only tiny, cheap jobs

You're reaching too-small businesses. Qualify on GMV/funding before calls; politely decline below your floor.

One big client fills your week

Keep the 90-minute outreach block anyway, or you'll restart from zero when it ends.

Runway under 3 months

Take a bridge contract (15–20 hrs/week) now, not when it's under 1 month. See bridge income.
