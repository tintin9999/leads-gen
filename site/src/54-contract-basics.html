For a direct client, nothing protects you except what you both agreed in writing. The rule is simple: one signed page and a deposit before any work. This page shows what to send, what the page must say, and which template to use.

The day she says yes

Asha runs "Indigo Thread", a made-up Pune clothing brand on Shopify. On Thursday she writes on WhatsApp: "Let's do the ₹48,000 option. When can you start?"

That same day, you send her two things by email:

  • The one-page agreement, filled in with the Store Speed Sprint scope, the price and the dates.
  • An invoice for the first half (the deposit): 50% of ₹48,000 = ₹24,000, due in 7 days.

Then you reply on WhatsApp:

Great, thank you Asha. I have emailed a one-page agreement and the deposit invoice for ₹24,000. Once you sign and the deposit arrives, I start the next working day.

What you do not do: open her store and start "quickly" to look keen. If she then changes her mind, you have worked for free.

A yes on WhatsApp is a good sign, not a contract. The signed page and the deposit turn it into a real job.

Most clients expect this. Asking for it calmly makes you look experienced, not difficult.

On Upwork you do not need any of this. The platform's terms already cover scope, payment and ownership. This page is only for clients you find yourself.

The six promises that matter most

A contract is just a list of promises. These six cause most fights when they are missing.

1. What is included, and what is not

List three to six things you will deliver, each with a way to check it. For Asha: "product pages load in under 3 seconds on a mid-range phone".

Then add one line: anything not listed is quoted separately. This line is your answer when she asks for "one small extra thing".

2. The price, and when it is due

Write the fixed price, ₹48,000. Then the split: ₹24,000 before work starts, ₹24,000 on delivery.

Each invoice is due in 7 days. Work starts when the deposit reaches your account, not when she says it was sent.

3. Who owns the code, and when

The legal right to copy and use the code (copyright) passes to her only when the final invoice is paid. Until then she may use the work only to review it.

In India the wording matters. The contract must say the rights pass "for the full term of copyright, worldwide".

Without those words, the law cuts them to 5 years and India only. The template already has this wording. Never remove it.

You keep your own tools and code you wrote before. She gets permission to use them as part of her project.

4. A cap on what you can owe

If something goes wrong, the most you can owe her is the fee she paid. For Asha, that is ₹48,000 at most.

Neither side pays for lost profits. Without a cap, one bad sale week could be blamed on you in full.

5. How either side can end it

Either of you can end the job by email. She pays for work done so far, and the deposit counts towards it.

You hand over the work in progress once that is paid. This keeps a bad project from dragging on.

6. Secrecy

Each side keeps the other's private information private (confidentiality). For you this means her store passwords, sales figures and customer data.

Never paste her customer data into AI tools. You may name her as a client, but a full write-up needs her approval.

A promise not to work for her competitors (a non-compete) is not valid in India. If a client asks for one, the template's line "nothing restricts Consultant from working for anyone else" stays in.

Which template to use when

One small job, up to about ₹50,000One-page agreement
Bigger job, or a client you will work with againMaster agreement plus a statement of work
A client outside IndiaInternational master agreement

The longer setup has two parts. The general rules (the master agreement) are signed once. Each new project then gets a short page of its own scope, price and dates (the statement of work).

Asha's ₹48,000 sprint fits the one-page agreement. If she comes back for monthly upkeep, move her to the master agreement.

Signing, stamp duty and lawyers

Signing electronically is fine. A free e-signature tool, or a reply saying "I agree to the Project Agreement above", is binding in India. Save the email thread.

In Maharashtra, a service agreement carries a small government fee (stamp duty) of ₹500, as of November 2026.

You do not need a lawyer for a ₹48,000 job with the template. Pay a lawyer for one review when:

  • A deal is above about ₹2 lakh or $5,000.
  • The client sends their own long contract.
  • The client wants a clause you do not understand, like unlimited liability.

One review of your master agreement can then be reused for years.
