Every proposal costs you money and about 20 minutes. So the job you pick matters more than the words you write. This page gives you a 2-minute check that says "apply" or "skip".

The 2-minute check, on a real post

Maya is a made-up client we use on every Upwork page. She is a US founder. Her app "Fernleaf" was built with an AI app builder on Next.js and Supabase.

Her post: "Fix login and Stripe checkout bugs in my Next.js + Supabase app". Fixed price, $300. You score it with one point for each signal below.

  1. Payment verified: yes, Upwork has checked her card. 1 point.
  2. Money spent: $4,000 on Upwork so far. She pays for work. 1 point.
  3. Hire rate: 9 hires from 12 posts, so 75%. She posts to hire, not to browse. 1 point.
  4. Reviews she gave: 4.8 stars from freelancers, and her own reviews of them are kind and specific. 1 point.
  5. Age of the post: 40 minutes. 1 point.
  6. Proposals so far: "5 to 10". Few people are ahead of you. 1 point.
  7. Clear task: two named bugs, one stack, one price. 1 point.
  8. Fit with your service: login and payments on Next.js and Supabase is exactly what you sell. 1 point.

Score: 8 out of 8. Apply now.

Use this rule for every post. A score of 6 to 8 means apply.

A score of 4 or 5 means apply only if the fit is perfect. A score of 0 to 3 means skip.

The signals sit in two places. The right side of the job page shows the client: payment, spend, hires and rating. The "Activity on this job" box shows the proposal count.

A post you skip

"Need expert to build full Uber clone app. Budget $50. Contact me on Telegram for details."

  • Payment not verified, $0 spent, no hires: 0 points.
  • No reviews given, posted 3 days ago, "50+" proposals: 0 points.
  • The task is vague, and $50 cannot pay for it: 0 points.
  • Fit: it is a new app, not a fix. 0 points.

Score: 0 out of 8. Skip, and do not think about it again.

Some things mean "skip" whatever the score. Skip if the post asks you to talk on Telegram, WhatsApp or email.

Skip if it wants free test work. Skip if it asks you to download and run their code before any contract.

Those last ones are often scams. See Is this client a scam? and A stranger asks you to run their code.

Save three searches for your service

A saved search shows you only the jobs that fit. You make it once and open it every time.

  1. Open Upwork and click Find work in the top menu.
  2. Type the first search words into the search box and press Enter.
  3. Open the filters next to the results.
  4. Under Experience level, tick Intermediate and Expert.
  5. Under Job type, tick Hourly with a minimum of $30, and Fixed price from $100 upwards.
  6. Under Number of proposals, tick Less than 5, 5 to 10 and 10 to 15.
  7. Under Client info, tick Payment verified.
  8. Under Client history, tick 1 to 9 hires and 10+ hires.
  9. Sort by newest, then click Save search and give it a name.
  10. Repeat for the other two searches.

Use these search words. Filter names can change a little, so pick the closest match.

  • Search 1: Supabase
  • Search 2: Lovable, Bolt, v0, Cursor (put them in "any of these words")
  • Search 3: Next.js Stripe, Next.js Razorpay, Next.js admin dashboard

When to look, from Pune

New posts matter most. In the first hour, few people have applied and the client is still reading.

In November 2026, US Eastern time is 10.5 hours behind India time (IST). So 9am in New York is 7:30pm in Pune.

London is 5.5 hours behind. So 9am in London is 2:30pm in Pune.

Check your saved searches three times a day, about 20 minutes each.

  • 7:30am IST: posts from the US evening, made while you slept.
  • 2:30pm IST: posts from the UK and Europe morning.
  • 8pm IST: posts from the US East Coast morning. This is often the best hour.

The US moves its clocks in March. From then, add one hour to the gap you just used, so New York 9am becomes 6:30pm in Pune.

Your weekly budget in Connects and rupees

You pay to apply with tokens called Connects. Each Connect costs $0.15, as of November 2026. Each job shows its own price in Connects, next to the button you click to apply.

Most jobs like Maya's ask for about 8 to 16 Connects. That range comes from freelancer reports, so treat it as rough. Always read the number on the job.

A boost is extra Connects you bid to move your proposal higher in the client's list. The page Write a proposal that gets a reply explains when to use one.

Proposals a week:  2 to 3 a day × 5 days = 10 to 15. Plan for 12.
Connects for them: 12 × about 12 Connects = 144 Connects
Small boost reserve:                       + 30 Connects
Weekly total:      144 + 30 = 174, round to 180 Connects
In dollars:        180 × $0.15 = $27 a week
In rupees:         $27 × ₹95 = ₹2,565 a week
In a month:        ₹2,565 × 4 = ₹10,260

This uses ₹95 to $1, so check the live rate. Tax may be added at checkout. The free plan also gives you 10 Connects a month.

If you run out before Friday, do not buy more. Spend the time on the job you already have, or on proof pieces.
