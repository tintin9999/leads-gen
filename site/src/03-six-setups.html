You do not need a company to start on Upwork. You need six small things, and most take under an hour each. This page lists them in order, then names what you can ignore for now.

Why these six

Say your first job pays $300. After Upwork's fees, about ₹25,000 reaches your bank.

To get there, Upwork must know who you are, where you pay tax, and which bank account is yours. That is setups 1 to 4.

Setups 5 and 6 protect you: one if you fall ill, one if someone steals a password.

Nothing else is needed for your first jobs. Upwork writes the contract and the invoice for you.

1 and 2: a work bank account and your documents

1. A bank account only for work money. Open a second savings account in your own name, with UPI.

A business account is not needed yet.

When only work money goes in, the bank statement is your income record. Tax time becomes simple. The account name must match your PAN and your Upwork name exactly.

2. Documents for Upwork's identity check. Keep these ready as clear photos:

  • Your PAN card. Check on the income-tax website that it is linked to Aadhaar.
  • A government photo identity document. A passport or driving licence is the safest choice.
  • A recent bank statement or bill that shows your address.

Upwork asks for a photo of the document and a live selfie or short video. Most checks finish in minutes. Some take up to 48 hours.

3: Upwork's tax forms

  1. Open the Tax information page in your Upwork account. Look under Settings, or under Finances and then Taxes.
  2. Fill in the US form called W-8BEN. It tells Upwork that you are not a US taxpayer.
  3. Enter your PAN in the India section.
  4. Leave the box for a Goods and Services Tax (GST) number empty.

Without the W-8BEN, you cannot withdraw. Upwork may also hold back up to 30% for the US tax office.

With a verified PAN, Upwork holds back 0.1% of each payment as Indian income tax. Without one, it holds back 5%. Verifying the PAN can take 7 working days, so do it now.

This is called tax deducted at source (TDS). It follows India's rule for online marketplaces, section 393(1) of the Income-tax Act 2025. The old number was section 194-O.

TDS is not lost. It counts as tax already paid when you file your return.

GST means India's Goods and Services Tax (GST). You have no GST number, so Upwork adds 18% GST to its own fee and to Connects, the tokens you buy to apply.

On a $300 job with a 10% fee, that GST is $5.40. Registering for GST only to save this is not worth it yet.

4: how money reaches your bank

In Upwork, open Settings, then the page for withdrawal methods. Add your work account. As of November 2026 you have two real choices in India.

MethodUpwork's feeHow long
Direct to Local Bank, in rupees$0.99 each timeWithin 4 working days
Wire transfer, in dollars$50 each timeUp to 7 working days

Choose Direct to Local Bank. You need your account number and the 11-character branch code (IFSC). A new method can be used after 3 days.

Payoneer is not offered to freelancers in India. PayPal, where shown, costs much more. The smallest withdrawal is $5 or more: check the figure on your withdrawal screen.

Later you may need proof that money came from abroad. Banks call it a foreign inward remittance advice (FIRA). Upwork does not make one itself.

You ask Upwork support, with the withdrawal's ID, date and rupee amount. It usually comes within 4 weeks. Getting paid from abroad says when you need it.

5 and 6: health cover and two-step login

5. Health insurance. Your employer's cover ended when you left in September.

One hospital stay can cost more than months of income.

A common shape is a ₹10 lakh policy plus a ₹50 lakh top-up. At your age expect roughly ₹9,000 to ₹16,000 a year in total. Compare three insurers.

Check three things: no limit on room rent, hospitals near you in Pune, and the waiting time for old illnesses.

6. Two-step login. This means a password plus a code from your phone.

Turn it on for Upwork, your email, GitHub and your bank.

Use an authenticator app instead of SMS where you can. Save the backup codes on paper. A stolen Upwork account can lose its earnings and its reviews.

You do not need these yet

  • GST registration. Needed when yearly income nears ₹20 lakh, or an Indian business client asks. See Service tax (GST): do you need it yet?
  • A company. Not before income is steady for months. See Becoming a business, one step at a time.
  • Your own contract. Needed for your first client outside a portal. See Contracts in plain words.
  • Invoices. Upwork makes them for Upwork jobs. For other clients, see Invoice a client and collect the money.
  • A website rebuild. Your site is live and good enough. Improve it after your first reviews.

Do this now

  • Open a second savings account with UPI, in the exact name on your PAN.
  • Check that your PAN is linked to Aadhaar, and photograph your documents.
  • Fill in the W-8BEN and your PAN on Upwork's Tax information page.
  • Add Direct to Local Bank as your withdrawal method.
  • Buy health cover, and turn on two-step login in four places.
