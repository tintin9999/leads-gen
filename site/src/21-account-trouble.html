An email from Upwork that says "not approved" or "on hold" feels like the end. It almost never is. This page takes the five problems new freelancers in India meet most, and gives the steps for each, in order.

Your profile was not approved

You submit your profile on a Monday night. On Tuesday an email says your profile was not approved and that you may update it and submit again.

You make three changes.

The title becomes "Next.js & Supabase Developer | I Fix and Finish Web Apps". The overview opens with Cultus and its 890+ users. The photo shows your face clearly, in daylight.

You also remove one portfolio link that led to your own website. You submit again on Tuesday evening.

Those changes fix most rejections. Upwork's support staff usually point to the same few causes.

  • A vague title or overview that does not say what you do.
  • A photo that is dark, far away, or not of your face.
  • Contact details, or a link to a page that has them, anywhere in the profile.
  • Spelling mistakes, or a required field left empty, such as your city.
  1. Read the email to the end. It sometimes names the exact problem.
  2. Open your profile and fix the title, overview and photo first.
  3. Search the profile for "@", "http" and phone digits. Remove what you find.
  4. Fill every empty field in your location and work history.
  5. Click Submit or Review application again.

Reviews often finish within about a day. Upwork promises no timeline, so wait 3 working days before you ask.

Sometimes the email says the marketplace has enough people with your skills. That is about demand, not about you. Sharpen the profile toward fixing Supabase and AI-built apps, then submit again.

The identity check is stuck

The message looks like "Verify your identity" at the top of every page. Until it is done, you cannot send proposals or withdraw money.

As of November 2026, you get 7 days to finish it, or your account may be put on hold. The review can take up to 48 hours.

  1. Open Settings, then Identity verification, and read your status.
  2. If the camera fails, close other apps that use it, such as Zoom.
  3. Restart the browser, or try Chrome if you used another one.
  4. Start on your computer and finish on your phone, if the page offers it.
  5. Use the same document and the exact name you used to sign up.
  6. If it still fails after 48 hours, contact support with a screenshot.

Upwork may later ask for one more document or a short video. That is a normal check, not a sign of trouble.

An account hold or review notice

The message says your account is "on hold" or "under review". A red bar may block proposals or withdrawals.

New accounts get these checks for ordinary reasons. Common causes are a login through an app that hides your location (VPN). Others are many fast proposals or a missing tax form.

  1. Do not open a second account. Upwork treats that as a serious breach.
  2. Read the notice and do exactly what it asks, nothing more.
  3. Check that your tax form and PAN are complete on the Tax information page.
  4. Turn off any VPN and log in from your usual place.
  5. Reply to the support ticket once, calmly, with the facts and screenshots.
  6. Keep a note of the date and ticket number.

Upwork gives no fixed timeline for reviews. If a week passes with no answer, reply once more on the same ticket.

A bank withdrawal fails

You withdraw $263 to your work account. Two days later the money returns, with a note that the bank rejected it.

The cause: your Upwork name is "Parth Kulkarni", but the bank account says "Kulkarni Parth S".

The name on your bank account, your PAN and your Upwork account must match. A wrong branch code (IFSC) causes the same failure.

  1. Open your bank's app and copy the account name exactly as it shows.
  2. In Upwork, open Settings, then the withdrawal methods page.
  3. Remove the old bank method and add it again with the exact name and IFSC.
  4. Wait the 3 days before a new method can be used.
  5. Withdraw again. The returned money is back in your Upwork balance.

If the name cannot match, ask your bank to correct the account name to match your PAN.

A warning for sharing contact details

The message says you shared contact information or asked to communicate outside Upwork. It often comes after a message held up by Upwork's filter.

The cause is usually small: an email in a proposal, "WhatsApp me", or a calendar link before a contract.

  1. Stop. Do not argue in the chat with the client.
  2. Read the message Upwork flagged and delete similar text from your templates.
  3. Reply to the warning once: say you understand the rule and will keep all talk on Upwork.
  4. From now on, share contact details only after a contract has started.

A first warning is a warning. Repeated sharing, or taking payment outside Upwork, can close your account for good.

If a client pushes to move off Upwork, read The client wants to leave Upwork.

Where to ask Upwork

  • Open any Upwork help page and click Contact support at the top. Logged in, you can open a ticket or chat.
  • Reply on the same ticket each time. A new ticket starts the queue again.
  • The Upwork Community forum helps with "is this normal?" questions. Never post your documents there.

Keep every reply polite and short. The person reading it did not cause the problem.

Do this now

  • Check that your bank account name matches your PAN letter for letter.
  • Search your profile and saved proposals for "@", "http" and phone digits.
  • Turn off your VPN for Upwork.
  • Save the "Contact support" page in your browser bookmarks.
