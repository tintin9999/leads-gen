Freelance money arrives unevenly. A fixed half hour on the first working day of each month keeps it under control. This page shows one month worked out, then the routine step by step.

One month, worked out

In November you receive ₹1,40,000, all from Upwork, so all of it is foreign money. You pay ₹6,000 for work costs: AI tools, Connects and internet. You have decided to pay yourself a fixed ₹75,000 a month.

Received this month                        ₹1,40,000
Tax reserve:  10% of ₹1,40,000            −   ₹14,000
Work costs (tools, Connects, internet)     −    ₹6,000
Your fixed pay to yourself                 −   ₹75,000
Left as a buffer for slow months           =   ₹45,000

Safety savings before                        ₹4,05,000
Add the buffer                             +   ₹45,000
Safety savings now                         = ₹4,50,000
Monthly need:  ₹75,000 + ₹6,000            =   ₹81,000
Months of savings left:  ₹4,50,000 ÷ ₹81,000 ≈ 5.6 months

The tax reserve is money you set aside now for tax later. At your level the income tax is probably ₹0, so why 10%?

  • Upwork and foreign clients hold back almost no tax for you, so nothing is prepaid.
  • A few good months can push you past ₹24 lakh a year, where tax starts.
  • If you do not need it, it moves to your savings after you file your return.

An Indian business client usually holds back 10% of your invoice as tax, called tax deducted at source (TDS). On those payments, reserve nothing extra. The page Income tax, step by step has the full sums.

The fixed pay is the most important line. In a good month you do not spend more. In a bad month the buffer pays you.

Here is a slow month, December, with only one small job:

Received this month                          ₹40,000
Tax reserve:  10% of ₹40,000               −  ₹4,000
Work costs                                 −  ₹6,000
Your fixed pay to yourself                 − ₹75,000
Short this month                           = −₹45,000
Taken from safety savings:  ₹4,50,000 − ₹45,000 = ₹4,05,000
Months of savings left:  ₹4,05,000 ÷ ₹81,000    = 5 months

Your pay stayed the same. Only the savings moved, and you can see by how much.

Three separate pots

Keep the money in three places, so you can see each one at a glance.

  1. Working account: every payment lands here, and work costs leave from here.
  2. Tax pot: a separate savings account or deposit. Move the reserve in on the day of your routine. Do not touch it until March.
  3. Safety savings: money for slow months, in a separate account or a deposit you can break. Aim for 6 months of your monthly need.

Your fixed pay goes from the working account to your personal account on the same day each month.

The routine, step by step

Do this on the first working day of the month, with your bank app, Upwork and your money sheet open. Use the monthly money close template.

  1. Open your working account statement for last month.
  2. Write each payment received in your money sheet: date, client, amount in rupees.
  3. Download Upwork's transaction history for the month and save it.
  4. Save the payment proof for every direct foreign payment, with its invoice, in that month's folder.
  5. Write each work cost in the sheet, with its receipt.
  6. Move 10% of foreign receipts into the tax pot.
  7. Move your fixed pay to your personal account.
  8. Move what is left, beyond a small amount for next month's costs, into safety savings.
  9. Divide safety savings by your monthly need. Write down the months left.
  10. Check for unpaid invoices more than 7 days late, and send a reminder.
  11. Export the data from your tools, such as the lead tracker (CRM) and invoice generator, and save a copy in cloud storage.

If the months left fall below 3, read No work this month and savings are falling.

The yearly dates, such as 15 March for advance tax, are in the compliance calendar.

What to hand a chartered accountant

Use a chartered accountant for your first return. After that you may file yourself if it feels easy.

Hand over these, once a year in May or June:

  • Your money sheet for April to March.
  • Upwork's yearly earnings report.
  • The proofs of foreign payment, and your invoices.
  • The salary form from Noovosoft for April to September 2026.
  • Any advance tax receipts.

In Pune, a simple return like yours costs about ₹1,500–5,000, as of November 2026. Once you register for service tax, a yearly package is about ₹15,000–40,000.

Health insurance is a fixed cost

You lost your employer's health cover when you left Noovosoft. Replace it with your own policy, and treat it like rent.

Health policy, ₹10 lakh cover                  about ₹7,000–12,000 a year
Extra cover above ₹10 lakh (super top-up)      about ₹2,000–4,000 a year
Total                                          about ₹9,000–16,000 a year
Per month:  ₹16,000 ÷ 12                       about ₹1,350

Personal health policies have had no Goods and Services Tax (GST), India's service tax, since 22 September 2025. Add the yearly cost to your work costs, and set aside the monthly share in the routine.

Do this now

  • Choose your fixed monthly pay and write it down.
  • Put a repeating calendar event: first working day, 30 minutes, "Money routine".
  • Copy the monthly money close template into your notes.
  • If you have no health policy yet, compare two this week.
