The route your dollars take to your bank can cost 1% or 9%. This page compares three routes with real numbers. It also shows which papers to keep, so the money counts as a clean export.

$2,000, three ways

You have $2,000 to bring home. All sums use ₹95 to $1. Check the live rate before you compare.

Route 1: Upwork's withdrawal to your Indian bank. The $2,000 is already "Available" in your Upwork balance, after Upwork's fee.

Withdrawal fee                             −     $0.99
Sent                                       = $1,999.01
In rupees:  $1,999.01 × 95                 = ₹1,89,906
Lost to the route                          =       ₹94

Upwork uses its own exchange rate. The screen shows the rupee amount before you confirm. It may be a little lower than the rate on Google.

PayPal costs about ₹14,000 more than a payment service on one $2,000 invoice. Use it only when a client cannot pay any other way, and add the cost to your price.

The proof of foreign payment

For each foreign payment, keep a paper that proves the money came from abroad for your service. It is called a Foreign Inward Remittance Advice (FIRA).

Payment services like Skydo send an e-FIRA free with each payment. Banks give one on request, sometimes for a fee. PayPal gives one each month.

Why keep it:

  • It proves the payment is export income, if the tax office asks.
  • Once you register for GST, it proves your foreign work is an export at 0%.
  • It helps your bank close its own records on the payment.

Save each FIRA in a folder by month, with the matching invoice.

Upwork's withdrawal may reach you as a local rupee transfer, so your bank may not issue a FIRA for it. Keep Upwork's earnings report and transaction history as your record. Ask a chartered accountant about this one.

The purpose code

Every foreign payment carries a short code that says what it is for. This is the purpose code.

For your work, use P0802, software consultancy and implementation. Payment services ask for it when you set up your account or send an invoice.

Never let a payment be tagged as a gift or as family support. That makes it look like something other than income from work.

The US form a client may ask for

Maya, a made-up founder in the US, hires you directly later. Her accountant emails: "Please send a W-8BEN before we pay."

A W-8BEN is a US form that says you are not a US person. With it, the client does not hold back US tax. Without it, they may hold back 24%.

Work you do from India is generally not US income, so no US tax should apply.

Fill in your name, India as country, your Indian address, and your PAN as "foreign tax number". Sign and date it. It stays valid until 31 December of the third year after you sign.

Never sign a W-9: that form is for US persons. On Upwork, you give this information once in the tax settings.

How long you have to bring money home

Under India's foreign exchange rules, money for exported services must reach India within 9 months of the invoice date. This is the rule from 1 October 2026.

With payment due in 7 days, this is rarely a problem. But chase any foreign invoice that is unpaid after 60 days.

Do not leave Upwork money sitting in your Upwork balance for months. Withdraw it each month.

Your choice, in plain words

  • Upwork money: use Upwork's direct withdrawal to your Indian bank, once a month. At $0.99 each time, it costs almost nothing.
  • A direct foreign client: use a low-cost payment service such as Skydo, with a free FIRA. Send the client its US, UK or EU account details, so they pay a local transfer, not an international wire.
  • A client who insists on PayPal or card: add 9% to the invoice, and say so in your quote.

Stripe is invite-only for new Indian accounts as of November 2026, so do not plan around it.

Before you pick a route for good, test it once with a small payment. Compare the rupees that reach your bank with the dollar amount sent. Keep whichever route gives you more.

Do this now

  • Check that your Upwork withdrawal method is your Indian bank account.
  • Make a folder called "Payment proofs" with one folder per month.
  • Download Upwork's transaction history for each month you were paid.
  • Check your W-8BEN details in Upwork's tax settings.
