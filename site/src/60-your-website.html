Your site at parthkulkarni.dev is live and it is good enough. This page says what the site is for and which changes are waiting for you. It also shows how to turn a case study into a file for Upwork.

What the site is for

A founder reads one of your backup emails. She clicks your name and lands on the site.

In 30 seconds she sees one line: "I build and fix the parts of your product that touch money."

Below it she sees four proofs: Cultus, Deribit, the tax module and the Bro bot. At the bottom she finds your email address.

That is the whole job of the site. It shows proof, and it gives one way to reach you.

Upwork clients rarely visit it. They read your Upwork profile and your reviews instead.

So the site matters most for the slow backup and for LinkedIn. It does not need more pages in Stage 1.

Check the branch and make it live

  1. Open your Vercel project and find the preview for the branch. Open it on your phone too.
  2. Click through the home page and the Cultus and Bro case studies.
  3. Decide about the two links. The next heading helps.
  4. To take everything, merge the whole branch into main.
  5. To take everything except the two links, merge only up to the fourth change.

If you cannot find a preview, run the site on your laptop. Type node scripts/serve.mjs in the portfolio folder and open the address it prints.

These are the commands for step 5. The code after --ff-only names the fourth change.

git checkout main
git pull
git merge --ff-only 6d2b3a7
git push

For step 4, use the branch name in place of that code. Vercel puts main live by itself after the push.

Save a case study as a file for Upwork

Upwork lets you add work samples to your profile. It does not allow contact details before a contract.

The print version leaves out your email, your links and the site menu. So the file is safe to upload.

  1. Open the case study in Chrome, for example the Cultus page.
  2. Press Ctrl and P together. On a Mac, press Cmd and P.
  3. Under "Destination", choose "Save as PDF".
  4. Choose A4 paper and leave the margins on "Default".
  5. Open "More settings". Tick "Background graphics".
  6. Untick "Headers and footers". This keeps the page address off the file.
  7. Click "Save". You get a file of two pages.

Do this for Cultus and for Bro first. Step 5 shows where the files go in your Upwork profile.

The file leaves out every link on purpose. In the Upwork item itself you can add the link to cultus.co.in, as step 5 shows.
