A month with no paid work is frightening. Fear makes people take bad jobs or quit too early. This page helps you count how many months your savings last, and act at fixed points instead.

Count the months your savings last

The number of months your savings can pay for your life is called your runway. You work it out with one division.

Say you have ₹3 lakh saved. You spend ₹45,000 a month on rent, food, bills and travel. This month, your freelance income is ₹0.

Savings                         ₹3,00,000
Spending each month             ₹45,000
Income each month               ₹0
Money lost each month           ₹45,000 − ₹0 = ₹45,000
Runway                          ₹3,00,000 ÷ ₹45,000 = 6.7 months

Now say a small Upwork job and some side work bring in ₹20,000 a month.

Savings                         ₹3,00,000
Spending each month             ₹45,000
Income each month               ₹20,000
Money lost each month           ₹45,000 − ₹20,000 = ₹25,000
Runway                          ₹3,00,000 ÷ ₹25,000 = 12 months

₹20,000 a month nearly doubles your runway. That is why small income matters early, even when it feels too small.

Use your real numbers. The savings tab of the rate calculator does this sum for you.

What is normal in the first weeks

Slow first weeks are expected. A new Upwork profile with no reviews often gets few replies for the first 2 to 4 weeks.

Many freelancers wait 4 to 8 weeks for the first paid job. A month at ₹0 does not mean the plan has failed. It means you are early.

What matters is whether you are doing the work that leads to jobs. If you sent 40 proposals and got no replies, read I sent 20 proposals and got no replies. The problem is often fixable.

Act at fixed trigger points

A trigger point is a runway level you choose now, while you are calm. When you reach it, you act, without a long debate.

  • 6 months or more: keep going with the plan. Put your hours into Upwork and proof pieces.
  • Under 6 months: add one side income of up to 15 to 20 hours a week.
  • Under 3 months: take a contract job, part-time or full-time, with steady pay. Keep at least 1 hour a day for Upwork.

In the first example, you start at 6.7 months. After one month with no income, you have ₹2,55,000 left.

Savings left                    ₹3,00,000 − ₹45,000 = ₹2,55,000
Runway                          ₹2,55,000 ÷ ₹45,000 = 5.7 months
5.7 is under 6                  → add one side income now

Check your runway on the first day of each month. Write the number down. Act only when it crosses a trigger point.

The spending side counts too

Runway has two sides: what comes in and what goes out. Cutting spending a little works as well as earning a little.

Say you cut ₹5,000 a month from eating out and subscriptions. Your income stays at ₹0.

Spending each month             ₹45,000 − ₹5,000 = ₹40,000
Runway                          ₹3,00,000 ÷ ₹40,000 = 7.5 months

One small cut gave you almost a month more. Do not cut what keeps you working well: internet, a working laptop, decent food and sleep.

Also keep business costs small for now. You do not need paid tools, a company or a logo yet. Upwork handles contracts and invoices for you in this stage.

Side income that suits you

Side income buys you time. It does not replace the plan. Choose one kind, and give it fixed hours, for example afternoons only.

Pay below is rough, from listings checked in November 2026. It changes often.

  • Freelance technical interviewer: you run job interviews for other companies, for example on BarRaiser. Roughly ₹1,500 to ₹2,000 an interview. You have run 5+ interviews already, and you meet engineering managers.
  • Part-time contract work: for example through Arc, Wellfound or an Indian startup. Roughly $35 to $40 an hour, or ₹1,200 to ₹1,500. It can also give you a case study.
  • AI training and code review work: for example Mercor or Outlier. Roughly $13 to $28 an hour for India listings. It pays in dollars quickly, but work comes and goes.
  • Paid mentoring: for example on Topmate. Roughly ₹999 to ₹2,499 a session. It is slow at first, because people need to find you.

More options and the sign-up pages are in the side income list.

Never pay to get work. A "registration fee", a "training kit" or a task app that asks for a deposit is a scam. See Is this client a scam?

Read any side contract before you sign. Do not accept a clause that stops you taking your own clients. Also refuse one that claims code you write outside the job.

When to stop the side income

Set the exit rule on the first day, so you do not drift. A simple one:

"I stop the side work when my freelance income covers my ₹45,000 spending for 2 months in a row."

Taking a contract job under 3 months is not failure. It protects your savings, and you can restart the plan with more runway later.

Do this now

  • Write down your real savings and monthly spending.
  • Work out your runway in the savings tab of the rate calculator.
  • Write your two trigger points and your exit rule on paper.
  • Add "check runway" to your calendar on the 1st of each month.
  • Pick the one side income you would start first, just in case.
