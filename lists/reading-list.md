# Reading list: the business side, fast

> **BLUF.** You don't need an MBA. Read these five first, in this order, about 15 hours in total: *The Mom Test* → *Don't Call Yourself a Programmer* (essay) → *The Positioning Manual* → *Hourly Billing Is Nuts* → *Never Split the Difference*. Everything else is optional.

## Core five (read in month one)

| # | Title | Author | Why it matters for you | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | *The Mom Test* | Rob Fitzpatrick | How to run discovery calls that surface real problems and budgets, not polite lies | 3 h |
| 2 | ["Don't Call Yourself A Programmer, And Other Career Advice"](https://www.kalzumeus.com/2011/10/28/dont-call-yourself-a-programmer/) | Patrick McKenzie | The mindset shift: you sell business outcomes, not code | 40 min |
| 3 | *The Positioning Manual for Technical Firms* | Philip Morgan | How to pick and own a niche as a technical freelancer | 3 h |
| 4 | *Hourly Billing Is Nuts* | Jonathan Stark | Why fixed and value pricing beat hourly; how to make the switch | 2 h |
| 5 | *Never Split the Difference* | Chris Voss | Negotiation without discounting: labels, calibrated questions, "no"-oriented questions | 6 h |

## Essays and free resources

- [Patrick McKenzie — Salary Negotiation](https://www.kalzumeus.com/2012/01/23/salary-negotiation/): the same principles apply to rate conversations.
- [Martin Fowler — Strangler Fig Application](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/StranglerFigApplication.html): your B3 migration pitch, in one page.
- [web.dev — Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals) and [Optimize LCP](https://web.dev/articles/optimize-lcp): A1 sprint fundamentals.
- [Shopify — App development docs](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps): sales channels, webhooks, checkout extensibility, Functions.
- [Razorpay — Magic Checkout docs](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/magic-checkout/): A3 offer.
- [Supabase — Going into production](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/deployment/going-into-prod) and [Row Level Security](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/database/postgres/row-level-security): B1 offer.
- [OWASP Top 10](https://owasp.org/www-project-top-ten/) and [ASVS](https://owasp.org/www-project-application-security-verification-standard/): structure for security audits.

## Podcasts / newsletters (pick one, not all)

- **Ditching Hourly** (Jonathan Stark): pricing and positioning for independent professionals.
- **Indie Hackers** (community + podcast): founders who might become B1 clients, and how they think.
- **Lenny's Newsletter**: how product teams think; useful for talking to startup clients.

## How to read for business (not for fun)

1. Read with a question: "What will I change in my outreach/pricing/proposals this week?"
2. After each book, write three changes into the relevant template in this repo.
3. Stop reading if you're behind on your daily 15 touches. Reading is not selling.
