# Compliance calendar: Pune-based freelancer (income tax, GST, PT, FEMA)

*As of Nov 2026. Tax year = 1 April – 31 March. Dates come from the Income-tax Act 2025, GST rules and Maharashtra rules as reported by the sources below. Confirm any date that a notification can move (marked ⚠) on the official portal.*

> **BLUF.** In year 1, before GST, you have about **six hard dates**: **31 Dec 2026** (belated FY 2025-26 return, if missed), **15 Mar 2027** (advance tax, single instalment under s.58), **15/30 Jun** (Maharashtra PTEC ⚠), **~31 Aug 2027** (ITR for tax year 2026-27 ⚠), plus a **quarterly AIS/Form 168 check** and a **monthly money close**. GST registration adds 8 returns a year (QRMP) and an annual LUT. Put every row marked "you" into your calendar today. Context: [Business Setup](../memos/02-business-setup.html) · [Tax & Money](../memos/03-tax-and-money.html).

## Penalties cheat sheet (why the dates matter)

| Miss | Cost |
|---|---|
| ITR after the due date | Late fee ₹5,000 (₹1,000 if income ≤ ₹5L), plus 1%/month interest on unpaid tax. You lose the right to carry forward some losses |
| Advance tax short by 15 Mar | About 1% (s.425). If less than 90% is paid by 31 Mar, 1% per month (s.424) until paid |
| GST return late | ₹50/day (₹20/day for nil returns), capped per return (₹500 for nil; ₹2,000 for turnover up to ₹1.5 Cr), plus **18% p.a. interest** on late tax |
| No LUT before exporting while registered | Exports become 18% IGST that you then have to claim back as a refund |
| PTEC late | About 1.25% per month interest. Penalties for not enrolling |
| Foreign proceeds not realised in 9 months | Your bank must report it; you need a write-off or extension through your AD bank |
