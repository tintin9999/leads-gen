# Bridge Income: Fund the Runway Without Killing the Pipeline (as of Nov 2026)

> **BLUF.** Bridge income buys you **time**, not a business. If your runway is **under 4 months** of expenses, add **one** bridge stream now and cap it at **≤ 20 hrs/week**. Mornings stay reserved for pipeline building. The best fits are **freelance technical interviewing**, which uses your 5+ interviews and puts you in front of hiring managers, and **part-time contract work** from Arc, Wellfound, or Indian startups. **AI-training/code-eval work** (Mercor, Outlier, Turing, micro1) pays USD fast but is volatile and builds no brand. Quit the bridge when retainers and project revenue cover expenses for two straight months.

## Rules
1. **≤ 20 hours/week, with a fixed schedule.** For example, AI-eval only in afternoons, and interviews only Tue/Thu evenings. Pipeline time ([memo 05 → Outreach OS](../memos/05-lead-generation.html#outreach-os)) is never the part you sacrifice.
2. **Set an exit trigger** on day 1: *"Stop when 2 consecutive months of client revenue ≥ ₹{{MONTHLY_EXPENSES}}."*
3. **Read the contract:** exclusivity, IP assignment, confidentiality, non-solicit. Don't sign anything that blocks your own clients or claims code you write outside the gig.
4. **Prefer options that compound.** Interviewing gives you a network, mentoring gives you content and authority, and contracts give you a case study. AI-eval gives you cash only.
5. **It's all taxable professional income**, and some of it arrives in USD. See [memo 03](../memos/03-tax-and-money.html) (presumptive taxation, foreign remittance paperwork).
6. **Never pay to get work.** No "registration fee", "training kit", crypto "task" apps, or Telegram "like and review" jobs. Those are scams ([memo 11](../memos/11-risks-and-scams.html)).

## Suggested combos by runway

| Runway (months of expenses saved) | Bridge plan |
|---|---|
| **≥ 6 months** | None. Put the 20 hours into outreach and portfolio. Optional: 2 mentoring slots/week. |
| **4–6 months** | Interviewing (3–4/week ≈ ₹20–30k/month at ₹1,500–2,000 each) + mentoring. |
| **2–4 months** | Interviewing + **one** AI-eval platform (Mercor/Outlier) capped at 12 h/week. |
| **< 2 months** | Take a part-time contract (20 h/week) now, keep a minimum of 1 h/day on pipeline, and reassess every 4 weeks. |

## Where to apply
- BarRaiser (interviewer marketplace): [barraiser.com](https://www.barraiser.com) → become an expert/interviewer.
- Mercor: [work.mercor.com](https://work.mercor.com). Filter to software/coding roles tagged India or worldwide.
- Outlier: [outlier.ai](https://outlier.ai). Search "coding expertise" listings for India.
- Turing, micro1, Alignerr: use the official sites only. Ignore referral-link blogs.
- Topmate: [topmate.io](https://topmate.io). Make one "React/Next.js mock interview" and one "Freelance-ready code review" offering.
- Part-time contracts: Arc.dev, Wellfound (contract filter), HN "Who is hiring?" (search "part-time").
